# Bridgestone Firestone: Best CSR Manufacturer Asia Pacific 2016

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `3795-bridgestone-firestone-best-csr-manufacturer-asia-pacific-2016.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-3796" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/BridgestoneFirestone.jpg" alt="" width="800" height="505" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The Bridgestone Firestone Group is the world’s largest tyre manufacturer since 2015. As the company grows, so too does its social responsibility.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The group has been working fervently to achieve its CSR vision through its core mission of “serving society with superior quality”; words famously uttered by Bridgestone’s founder Shojiro Ishibashi.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The company’s two-pronged approach to CSR includes a focus on both management level – through its products and corporate activity – and individual level – through employee activities. However, Bridgestone Firestone’s larger initiatives surrounding mobility in Asia and the Pacific have been the most impressive: the company’s involvement in the Bangkok Traffic Congestion Elimination Project is often cited as an example.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Bridgestone Firestone’s stance on the development of mobility sees a recycling-orientated society as a necessity. Through establishing the OneTeam environmental programme the company has developed a framework where both leadership and teammates are focused on methods to achieve the company’s environmental goals.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Because of this, Bridgestone Firestone Americas has moved forward with its vision of achieving a waste-free tyre industry under the Tires4ward programme. With this, the company aims to ensure that for every tyre that it sells in the US, it will repurpose an out-of-use tyre. Since the programme launched in 2012, Bridgestone Firestone has recycled almost 120,000 tyres from communities nationwide.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co judging panel commends Bridgestone Firestone on its deep involvement with the development of mobility alongside CSR and offers the company the 2016 Best CSR Manufacturer Asia Pacific Award.</p>
