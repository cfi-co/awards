# Vestas Wins Corporate Governance Award in Denmark

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `1203-vestas-wins-corporate-governance-award-in-denmark.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1204" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/Vestas.jpg" alt="Vestas" width="425" height="85" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">R<strong>epeating their success of last year, Vestas is named winner of the award ‘Best Corporate Governance, Denmark, 2014’.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Vestas is to be congratulated on a second consecutive year win in a country where the overall standard of corporate governance is indeed very high.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Once again the Judges pointed out that, ‘this is a most transparent organisation that stands ahead of the competition in a very competitive field. Vestas does many important things extremely well and the level of corporate governance is exemplary.’</p>
