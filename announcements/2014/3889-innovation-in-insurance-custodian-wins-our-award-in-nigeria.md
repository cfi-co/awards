# Innovation in Insurance: Custodian Wins our Award in Nigeria

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `3889-innovation-in-insurance-custodian-wins-our-award-in-nigeria.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-3890" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2017/03/Custodian.jpg" alt="" width="198" height="189" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Custodian and Allied PLC, winner of the 2014 CFI.co award for ‘Most Innovative Insurance Company, Nigeria’, provides good evidence of this important characteristic with the recent launch of a mobile application that guides the customer through the complexities of the complete insurance cycle. </strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Innovation in risk management is evidence too and the insurer has a good response time record in respect of claims payment.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">There is an attitude at Custodian of seeking out innovation to drive the best possible methods of service delivery.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Judging Panel was also impressed with this company’s highly innovative approach and intelligent actions to encourage sustainability and described Custodian as a most thoughtful corporate citizen.</p>
