# First Registrars Wins the CFI.co Award for Best Share Registrar, Nigeria

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `7262-first-registrars-wins-the-cfi-co-award-for-best-share-registrar-nigeria.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-7263" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/First-Registrars.jpg" alt="First Registrars" width="149" height="169" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The CFI.co Judging Panel has named First Registrars Nigeria Limited, one of 25 registration companies in the country, as winner of the 2014 award for ‘Best Share Registrar, Nigeria.’</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">According to the panel, ‘First Registrars certainly has the edge with its highly experienced team and very strong products and services. Customer care at First is exemplary. Formerly a wholly owned subsidiary of First Bank of Nigeria Plc, good corporate governance is a key focus of operations here and our winner offers an outstanding share/bond register administration service. First is indeed a very worthy winner of this award’.</p>
