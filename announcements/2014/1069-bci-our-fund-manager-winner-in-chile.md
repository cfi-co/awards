# BCI: Our Fund Manager Winner in Chile

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `1069-bci-our-fund-manager-winner-in-chile.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1142" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2014/01/bci1.jpg" alt="bci" width="531" height="141" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The CFI.co Judging Panel rates BCI Investment Management very highly and has announced that the award for ‘Best Fund Manager, Chile, 2013’ goes, with their congratulations, to this company. </strong>BCI works to high levels of operational efficiency and has strong risk management capabilities. According to the CFI.co Judging Panel, ’BCI ends the year on a high note providing them with a strong start to 2014. This firm has a good track record over the years and a history of strong and consistent portfolio managers. We wish them well.’</p>
