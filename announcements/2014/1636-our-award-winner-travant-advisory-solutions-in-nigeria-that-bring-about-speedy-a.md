# Our Award Winner Travant: Advisory Solutions in Nigeria that bring about Speedy and Positive Change

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `1636-our-award-winner-travant-advisory-solutions-in-nigeria-that-bring-about-speedy-a.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1637" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/travant.jpg" alt="travant" width="149" height="69" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The CFI.co Judging Panel is happy to recognise Travant Capital Partners as ‘Best Fund Advisory Team, Nigeria, 2014’. </strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Travant advises across a broad range of industries and has a strong client base. The panel comments that, ‘Travant, working to international best practice, shows ingenuity and innovation and tries to make a real difference to client businesses with solutions that can be implemented effectively to bring speedy and positive change. Here is a successful marriage of local knowledge and international expertise which results in a good understanding of the problems clients face. We congratulate Travant on this well-deserved award’.</p>
