# BNY Mellon: Winner of the CFI.co Wealth Manager Award, US

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `1295-bny-mellon-winner-of-the-cfi-co-wealth-manager-award-us.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1296" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/bny.jpg" alt="bny" width="262" height="126" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The CFI.co Judging Panel has named BNY Mellon as winner of the prestigious award, ’Best Wealth Manager, Unites States, 2014’.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Client retention at Mellon is strong and the Bank is a superb relationship manager. This truly outstanding name in North American banking, which can trace its heritage back 230 years, is the result of a 2007 merger between Bank of New York and Mellon Finance.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The panel congratulates BNY Mellon, the largest deposit bank in the world, on its outstanding focus on and great success in providing the most sophisticated wealth management services.</p>
