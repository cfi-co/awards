# Riyad Bank Wins CFI.co Trade Finance Award

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `1289-riyad-bank-wins-cfi-co-trade-finance-award.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/rb.jpg"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1290" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/rb.jpg" alt="rb" width="400" height="123" /></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Riyad Bank has dedicated trade finance centres at each of the three main commercial cities namely Jeddah, Riyadh and Damman. </strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Each centre offers large bank benefits to clients and according to the Judging Panel, ‘can be relied upon to offer good advice, appropriate trade facilitation products and very helpful services to its roster of appreciative clients. The Panel has no hesitation in naming Riyad Bank as ‘Best Trade Finance Team, Saudi Arabia, 2014.’</p>
