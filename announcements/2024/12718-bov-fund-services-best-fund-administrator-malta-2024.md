# BOV Fund Services: Best Fund Administrator Malta 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12718-bov-fund-services-best-fund-administrator-malta-2024.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12720" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/BOV-Fund-Services-jpg.webp" alt="BOV Fund Services" width="500" height="92" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>BOV Fund Services (BOVFS) is a leading Maltese provider of fund administration services, renowned for its client-focused and tailored solutions. As a subsidiary of Bank of Valletta, the largest banking group in Malta, BOVFS combines local expertise with a personalised approach, prioritising the unique needs of each client.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The company empowers its employees through continuous training and development, ensuring that the team is equipped to respond effectively to client needs. This proactive approach is supported by a commitment to regulatory compliance and risk management, crucial in maintaining the high standards required in the financial services industry. BOVFS actively listens to its clients and adjusts its services accordingly, avoiding a one-size-fits-all model in favour of customised solutions that ensure long-term success and sustainability.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Corporate social responsibility is embedded in the company’s culture, with initiatives such as voluntary work and donations to local and international charities. Looking ahead, BOVFS is focused on organic growth, maintaining its strong market position by continually enhancing its technological infrastructure and adapting to evolving market conditions and client expectations. The company is committed to staying ahead of industry changes while remaining agile enough to adopt new technologies when necessary. This, combined with its long-standing commitment to quality service, positions BOVFS as a trusted partner for its clients in the evolving fund management landscape.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates BOV Fund Services on winning the 2024 award for Best Fund Administrator (Malta).</p>
