# Mubasher Capital: Best Financial Trading Platform Middle East 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12484-mubasher-capital-best-financial-trading-platform-middle-east-2024.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12486" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Mubasher-Capital-jpg.webp" alt="Mubasher Capital" width="600" height="136" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Mubasher Capital, established in Bahrain in 2007, has continually demonstrated its commitment to innovation and excellence in the financial trading sector. The company, licensed as a Category A investment company, offers an extensive range of financial services, including trading across 87 global markets, fixed income solutions, and treasury bills. Over the past year, Mubasher Capital has strategically transformed its objectives, focusing on becoming a leading investment firm. This strategic shift has not only expanded the company's market presence but also enabled the integration of cutting-edge technologies, significantly enhancing overall operational efficiency. One of the company's most notable achievements is the launch of its portfolio management division. This new division has delivered impressive results, allowing Mubasher Capital to engage more effectively with its clients and enrich their investment experience. The company's proprietary trading platform supports a wide range of trading activities and serves a diverse client base that includes investors from over a hundred nationalities. Mubasher Capital is committed to further enhancing its platform by incorporating artificial intelligence, focusing on robo-advisory and algorithmic trading. These advancements are expected to provide clients with unparalleled access to market insights and opportunities, reducing the time and effort required to make informed investment decisions. This forward-thinking approach ensures that Mubasher Capital remains at the forefront of the financial trading industry. The CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates Mubasher Capital on winning the 2024 award for Best Financial Trading Platform (Middle East).</p>
