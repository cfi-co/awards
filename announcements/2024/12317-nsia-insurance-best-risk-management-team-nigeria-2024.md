# NSIA Insurance: Best Risk Management Team Nigeria 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12317-nsia-insurance-best-risk-management-team-nigeria-2024.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12318" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NSIA-Insurance-jpg.webp" alt="NSIA Insurance" width="400" height="400" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">NSIA Insurance is a long-established and highly respected player in the Nigerian insurance market. Headquartered in Victoria Island, Lagos, it has offices in Abuja, Ikeja, Ibadan, Port Harcourt, Kaduna, Kano, Warri and Onitsha. Licensed by the National Insurance Commission (NAICOM), NSIA in Nigeria benefits from its association with NSIA Participations, a large regional insurance group, that provides additional resources, expertise, and further depth of experience. According to the judging panel, this winner, “exhibits a marked, effective, and satisfying client focus, providing tailored solutions for individuals and corporate entities, developing new services, and simplifying the claims process. NSIA core values include care to all stakeholders, innovation, transparency, and professionalism. It offers a comprehensive range of life and non-life insurance products and services. Moruf Apampa is managing director. He is an Insurance graduate from Lagos State Polytechnic and holds an MBA from the University of Ado-Ekiti. He has a stellar track record in the industry and is a fellow of the Chartered Insurance Institute of Nigeria. Speaking on the arrival of the New Year, Apampa promised that his team would remain committed to delivering personalised solutions, harnessing digital advancement, and maintaining the highest standards of service. He promised to focus on setting new benchmarks in the insurance industry. The panel has confidence in these words and wishes continued good progress for the firm. The judges have no hesitation in confirming NSIA Insurance as the 2024 winner of the award Best Risk Management Team (Nigeria).</p>
