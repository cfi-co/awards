# NBG Asset Management: Pioneer in Innovative Fund Solutions Greece 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12666-nbg-asset-management-pioneer-in-innovative-fund-solutions-greece-2024.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12667" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/NBG-AM-jpg.webp" alt="NBG AM" width="500" height="89" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Consistent with its pioneering approach to solutions, NBG Asset Management was among the first to introduce bond mutual funds with fixed maturity to the Greek financial market. These funds aim to deliver the initial capital a specific return upon expiration. As of today, NBG Asset Management has launched the largest number of such funds compared to its competitors. The firm has achieved a remarkable 84 percent growth rate in assets under management since the end of 2022, setting a benchmark in the industry. With a robust risk management approach, NBG Asset Management expertly navigates the complexities of the global financial landscape, including high public debt levels and the challenges of globalisation. The company’s diversified fund portfolio, which includes a Greek equites fund with cumulative return 119% and a global equities fund with a 75% over the last 5 years, exemplifies its commitment to delivering exceptional returns to its clients under varying market conditions. Client satisfaction is a cornerstone of NBG Asset Management’s success, reflected in a 100 percent satisfaction rate and zero complaints regarding fund management. The firm has garnered significant accolades, including recognition from Wall Street Journal as the best bond mutual fund in the world, and various national awards for profitability and growth. These achievements underscore NBG Asset Management’s position as a top contender in the European financial awards circuit. An innovative approach and consistent performance have made it a preferred choice among investors seeking reliable high-yield investment opportunities. The company continues to set standards through its commitment to excellence, innovation, and client satisfaction. The CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates NBG Asset Management on the 2024 award for Pioneer in Innovative Fund Solutions (Greece).</p>
