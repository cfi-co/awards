# Federal Realty Investment Trust: Best ESG Real Estate Investment Strategy US 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12476-federal-realty-investment-trust-best-esg-real-estate-investment-strategy-us-2024.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-10356" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Federal.jpg" alt="Federal" width="600" height="177" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">This award to Federal Realty Investment Trust marks a significant milestone in its journey towards sustainable real estate investment practices. The judging panel is pleased to confirm that, “Federal’s considered and effective approach to ESG integration in real estate investment is comprehensive.”  It is underpinned by a meticulously crafted programme that features five fundamental pillars designed to navigate the complex landscape of environmental, social, and governance considerations. Notably, the company underscores the importance of attention to detail as a cornerstone of strategy implementation, recognising its pivotal role in ensuring the efficacy and integrity of its initiatives. The issuance of a green bond stands as testament to an unwavering commitment to environmentally sustainable investment practices, demonstrating a proactive stance in financing projects with positive environmental impacts. Federal Realty Investment Trust's steadfast dedication to ESG is deemed essential not only for achieving its sustainability objectives but also for maintaining its leading position within the highly competitive real estate industry. Furthermore, this independent verification solidifies Federal Realty Investment Trust's standing as an industry trailblazer: the largest publicly traded shopping centre company in the United States in terms of solar installations and enhancing the credibility of sustainability initiatives. Federal Realty Investment Trust's multifaceted approach to ESG integration exemplifies its unwavering commitment to driving positive change and fostering sustainability in real estate investment across the United States. The CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates Federal Realty Investment Trust on the well-deserved 2024 award Best ESG Real Estate Investment Strategy (US).</p>
