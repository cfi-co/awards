# Old Mutual Zimbabwe: Outstanding Contribution to Financial Empowerment Zimbabwe 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12505-old-mutual-zimbabwe-outstanding-contribution-to-financial-empowerment-zimbabwe-2.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12507" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/OldMutual-jpg.webp" alt="OldMutual" width="500" height="110" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Old Mutual Zimbabwe is a prominent player in the financial sector, demonstrating unwavering commitment to financial literacy and empowerment across the nation. The company has developed and implemented a range of innovative financial products and services tailored to meet the diverse needs of Zimbabweans. These initiatives include extensive financial education programmes aimed at improving financial inclusion and literacy among underserved communities. Additionally, Old Mutual Zimbabwe has invested significantly in digital financial solutions, enhancing accessibility and convenience for customers. The company’s efforts extend to providing comprehensive insurance and investment services that promote economic stability and growth. Through partnerships with local businesses and international entities, Old Mutual Zimbabwe supports entrepreneurial ventures and sustainable development projects, further contributing to the nation's economic progress. Its commitment to corporate social responsibility is evident in its active participation in community development initiatives, focusing on education, health, and environmental sustainability. The company's Value Creation Challenge has been particularly impactful, fostering innovation and entrepreneurship across the country. Old Mutual Zimbabwe also plays a crucial role in the nation’s economic resilience by continuously adapting to market demands and supporting local talent. The company’s resilience in navigating economic challenges while maintaining a customer-centric approach has solidified its reputation as a leader in financial empowerment. Its dedication to fostering a sustainable and inclusive financial ecosystem is a testament to visionary leadership. The CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates Old Mutual Zimbabwe on winning the 2024 award for Outstanding Contribution to Financial Empowerment (Zimbabwe).</p>
