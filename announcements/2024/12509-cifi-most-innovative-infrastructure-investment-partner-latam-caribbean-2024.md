# Cifi: Most Innovative Infrastructure Investment Partner LATAM & Caribbean 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12509-cifi-most-innovative-infrastructure-investment-partner-latam-caribbean-2024.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12510" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Cifi-jpg.webp" alt="Cifi" width="231" height="135" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Founded in 2001 to cater to the underserved middle market of infrastructure in Latin America and the Caribbean, Cifi is a leader in innovative infrastructure investment. Operating from a base of experience and a network of strategic alliances with multilateral and private financial institutions, Cifi has carved a niche in project finance that transcends traditional lending. Its approach combines robust financial support with strategic advisory services, ensuring that each project is not only financially viable but also socially and environmentally sustainable. Cifi's expertise extends to the creation and management of targeted funds, such as local currency funds designed to mitigate exchange rate risks. These funds demonstrate Cifi's commitment to local economies while minimising exposure to volatility. A hallmark of Cifi’s strategy is its deep engagement with institutional clients, advising them on channelling their investments into projects that offer stable returns and positive societal impacts. This role has become increasingly critical as these investors seek not only financial gains but also opportunities to contribute to sustainable development within their regions. Additionally, Cifi is at the forefront of new technologies, such as the pioneering use of wind and solar energy in Guatemala a now successfully exited project to current solar distributed energy projects in Brazil and Mexico, positioning them as leaders in renewable energy within the infrastructure sector. Its commitment to sustainability is further endorsed via accreditation by the Green Climate Fund, underscoring its alignment with global environmental goals. CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates Cifi on winning the 2024 award Most Innovative Infrastructure Investment Partner (LATAM &amp; Caribbean).</p>
