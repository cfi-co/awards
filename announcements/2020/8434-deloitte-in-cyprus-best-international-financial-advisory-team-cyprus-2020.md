# Deloitte in Cyprus: Best International Financial Advisory Team Cyprus 2020

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `8434-deloitte-in-cyprus-best-international-financial-advisory-team-cyprus-2020.json`.

[caption id="attachment_8612" align="aligncenter" width="873"]<img class="size-full wp-image-8612" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Deloitte-CFI.co-Award-Win-2020.jpg" alt="Nicos Kyriakides - Deloitte-CFI.co-Award-Win-2020" width="873" height="582" /> Nicos Kyriakides[/caption]
<p style="text-align: justify;">Deloitte is a truly global organisation, with presence in more than 150 locations worldwide. Clusters of regional teams provide a comprehensive range of audit and assurance, consulting, financial advisory, risk advisory, and tax &amp; legal related services. Deloitte Limited is the sub-licensed affiliate of Deloitte North South Europe for Cyprus, supporting national and international clients with comprehensive advisory services. The Financial Advisory team in Cyprus brings with it vast cross-industry experience in shipping and ports, renewable energy, healthcare, real estate, banking and financial services, tourism and infrastructure. Deloitte in Cyprus has frequently served as a governmental adviser, providing financial due-diligence and regulatory support. As part of a team with Deloitte UK and external legal advisors, it helped to establish the required legal &amp; regulatory framework for Cyprus’ first – and Europe’s largest – Integrated Casino Resort (ICR) project. The firm has provided corporate finance and advisory services to develop, finance and attract investors for large-scale real estate projects in Cyprus including marinas and golf courses. It also supported funds and foreign investors to acquire and invest in portfolios of NPLs and distressed assets in the island. Deloitte in Cyprus cultivates a collaborative culture by investing in its people, supporting their development and rewarding their contribution. It makes continuous upgrades to its IT and operating infrastructure to improve on efficiency, economy and sustainability. The multi-disciplinary firm measures its success by a triple bottom-line: people, profit and planet. It is committed to action on climate change, responsible sourcing, environmental sustainability, gender equality and inclusivity. Deloitte in Cyprus impressed the CFI.co judging panel, and has been granted the 2020 award for Best International Financial Advisory Team (Cyprus).</p>
