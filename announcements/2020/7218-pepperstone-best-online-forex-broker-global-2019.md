# Pepperstone: Best Online Forex Broker Global 2019

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `7218-pepperstone-best-online-forex-broker-global-2019.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-7219" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Pepperstone.jpg" alt="Pepperstone" width="600" height="136" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Burned by lagging executions, high prices, and lacklustre support, a team of seasoned traders banded together to present a new offering in the world of online trading — Pepperstone. Over the past decade, it has registered remarkable growth globally. As the growth principally comes from word-of-mouth referrals, the broker can divert marketing funds to in-depth market analysis and award-winning customer support. Clear communication is a requisite for smart trading and good service, so Pepperstone speaks in a language that clients understand. On Pepperstone’s webpage, customers can live-chat with a team member in one of eight languages: English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian and Mandarin. For high-volume traders, Pepperstone offers a premium support service that features cash rebates and exclusive benefits to professional traders. A dedicated team of analysts covers global markets to create handy economic calendars, report on high-impact news, and produce insightful economic outlooks. Regulated by the UK Financial Conduct Authority and the Australian Securities and Investments Commission, Pepperstone averages $12.55bn worth of trades each day and is trusted by more than 57,000 traders worldwide. Pepperstone traders have access to a wide range of markets and instruments (forex, index and share CFDs, commodities, <a href="https://cfi.co/tag/crypto/">cryptocurrencies</a>, and currency index CFDs). Forex (FX) traders appreciate Pepperstone’s low spreads and lightning-fast executions as well as the deep liquidity afforded from multiple-sourced pricing. Pepperstone supplies FX traders with the tools and support to access — and successfully navigate — a market with a global trading volume of about $1.5tr a day. The CFI.co judging panel is delighted to present Pepperstone with the 2019 award for Best Online Forex Broker (Global).</p>
