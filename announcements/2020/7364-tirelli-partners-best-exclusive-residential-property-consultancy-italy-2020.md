# Tirelli & Partners: Best Exclusive Residential Property Consultancy Italy 2020

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `7364-tirelli-partners-best-exclusive-residential-property-consultancy-italy-2020.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-7365" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Tirelli.jpg" alt="Tirelli" width="500" height="164" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Tirelli &amp; Partners’ property book reads like the setlist of modern fairy tales: a Roman penthouse with a two-tiered terrace, a Tuscan villa complete with vineyard and winery, a Sardinian resort with stables, paddocks and riding school. Tirelli &amp; Partners provides high-quality, high-touch care and customised consultancy and intermediary services for exclusive residential and commercial properties. Clients have praised Tirelli for its professionalism, assistance and advice, from conception and product-planning to marketing and commercial initiatives. Furthermore Tirelli &amp; Partners set the standard with the quality of its marketing material. Using the highest quality and cutting edge technology, its virtual and augmented reality and trackable application provides its clients with the best results.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">As a globally certified B Corporation and an Italian Benefit Company, Tirelli &amp; Partners aspires to more than profit margins. The B-Corp designation refers to 3,200 companies — in over 150 sectors and 70 countries — that have pledged to follow a “non-extractive” business model that safeguards the environment and provides shared benefits for all. In 2019, it established the non-profit Cambia Mente (Changing Mindsets) Association to pursue, promote and propagate the benefits of responsible capitalism. The CFI.co judging panel believes in this new reality of shared prosperity and progress, and declares Tirelli &amp; Partners as the 2020 winner of the Best Exclusive Residential Property Consultancy (Italy) award.</p>
