# Boston Consulting Group (BCG): Best Mobility Innovation Experts Global 2019

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `7409-boston-consulting-group-bcg-best-mobility-innovation-experts-global-2019.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-7417" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/BCG.jpg" alt="BCG" width="413" height="168" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Boston Consulting Group’s legacy started in 1963, when founder Bruce Henderson challenged the status quo with his transformational business strategies. BCG introduced the growth-share matrix and time-based competition concepts that are now an accepted part of the business canon. The firm continues to innovate, partnering with leaders in business and society to tackle the most important challenges.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">BCG has focused its collective power— with 21,000 employees in 50 countries — on revolutionising the mobility industry. It works alongside public and private sectors as they experiment with innovative modes of transport and services around the world. It encourages companies to consider their purpose and helps them to articulate it for more efficient operations and better results. BCG forges dynamic relationships characterised by understanding and shared values that align commercial success with sustainability — and with its partners shapes mobility trends to create a roadmap for the future. The group promotes transition to a carbon-neutral world in which mobility and transport will play crucial roles. . As new technologies disrupt the market, BCG is there to help both entrepreneurs and established companies to seize fresh opportunities as they appear. Electric and self-driving cars are an increasing presence on the road, and navigation systems process caches of real-time data to keep traffic flowing. Boston Consulting Group pushes the tipping point of industry transformation —and the CFI.co judging panel recognises its contributions with the 2019 award for Best Mobility Innovation Experts (Global).</p>
