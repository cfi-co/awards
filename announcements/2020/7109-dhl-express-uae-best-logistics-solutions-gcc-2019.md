# DHL Express UAE: Best Logistics Solutions GCC 2019

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `7109-dhl-express-uae-best-logistics-solutions-gcc-2019.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-7110" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/DHL.jpg" alt="DHL" width="400" height="71" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Choosing a solid logistics partner is a crucial business decision in today’s interconnected world. DHL Express UAE boasts a global network of over 220 countries with more than 1.5 billion parcels delivered each year. The company’s 2020 strategy focuses on what it already does best: delivering excellence on a global scale with local insight. In the United Arab Emirates, certain significant challenges exist — including unclear addresses, postcode problems, and skyscraper deliveries — and local knowledge of drivers is key. The DHL Express UAE family is comprised of more than 1,200 motivated professionals with a deep understanding of the market. DHL Express UAE has received a resounding stamp of approval as an employer. It reports less than five percent staff turnover and wins the top employer award in the Middle East year after year. It continues to invest in technology and infrastructure to maximise efficiency and boost profits. The company has identified four trends shaping the industry — globalisation, digitalisation, e-commerce, and sustainability — and has laid out a plan to harness that momentum. The CFI.co judging panel has no reservations in declaring DHL Express UAE as 2019 winner of the Best Logistics Solutions (GCC) award.</p>
