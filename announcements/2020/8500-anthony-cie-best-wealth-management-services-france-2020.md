# Anthony & Cie: Best Wealth Management Services France 2020

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `8500-anthony-cie-best-wealth-management-services-france-2020.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-8501" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Anthony-and-Cie.jpg" alt="Anthony &amp; Cie" width="400" height="131" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Over the past four decades, Anthony &amp; Cie has proven that risk can be reconciled with opportunity. The multi-family office offers a suite of services that includes wealth planning, real estate and administration, and cross-border wealth strategy. The firm has Anglo origins, established in London in 1978, but has called the Côte d’Azur home for more than 30 years. Anthony &amp; Cie works with French residents as well as non-residents with local interests, such as foreign family offices. It has assembled a multinational, multilingual team of accountants, legal and tax advisors, financial analysts and administrative managers to provide clients with targeted advice. Founder Robert Anthony is a chartered certified accountant who previously taught international tax law in California. As principal and founder, Anthony leads from the front with a vigilant team ready to strike pre-emptively to hedge risk and seize opportunities. Client interests serve as the firm’s guiding star, and it regularly reviews and fine-tunes portfolios. Anthony &amp; Cie has an aversion to inappropriate speculation and a fondness for diversification. It strives to set a benchmark of exemplary business ethics and prizes retrospection as a tool for continuous improvement. The firm has positioned itself at the cutting edge of its field for legislation, relationships and knowledge. It has never been content with stasis, and the CFI.co judging panel watches with interest the firm’s evolution. The judges announce Anthony &amp; Cie as winner of the 2020 award for Best Wealth Management Services (France).</p>
