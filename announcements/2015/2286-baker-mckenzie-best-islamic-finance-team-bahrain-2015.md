# Baker & McKenzie: Best Islamic Finance Team Bahrain 2015

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `2286-baker-mckenzie-best-islamic-finance-team-bahrain-2015.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-2287 size-full" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/bm.jpg" alt="bm" width="400" height="91" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>In business since 1949, Baker &amp; McKenzie is right at home in a shrinking world: since its very inception, the company thinks, acts, and works on a global scale. With a fee-generated revenue in excess of $2.5bn in 2014, Baker &amp; McKenzie maintains a presence in 47 countries. The firm employs over 11,000 people in its 77 offices from which it serves – with notable distinction – more than 500 corporate clients who represent some of the world’s largest companies.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Baker &amp; McKenzie is an industry partner to the World Economic Forum (WEF). Earlier this year, the company’s chairperson, Eduardo Leite, co-chaired the 10<sup>th</sup> regional WEF conference in Latin America.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In Bahrain, Baker &amp; McKenzie ranks at the very top in several industry sectors and is widely recognised as an expert on Islamic finance. The CFI.co judging panel wholeheartedly agrees that the firm’s Islamic finance team stands out from the competition and has continued to power ahead convincingly since receiving an earlier award from CFI.co.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">For the past thirty years, Baker &amp; McKenzie has been a key player in the Middle East. The firm has been present in Bahrain since 1998. The company’s deep knowledge and understanding of the region remains unequalled. This breadth of experience coupled to the readily deployable – and massive – resources and its global footprint, make the Baker &amp; McKenzie team the one to watch for clues and cues. Over the years, the firm has been involved in many hallmark Islamic finance projects. The training and expertise of its lawyers has proved absolutely superb.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co judging panel is delighted to confirm, yet again, the leadership position of the Baker &amp; McKenzie team and declares the firm winner of the 2015 Best Islamic Finance Team Bahrain Award.</p>
