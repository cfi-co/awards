# Arntzen de Besche’s Energy Team Wins 2013 Legal Award

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>This outstanding Norwegian law firm offers a wide range of services across corporate law and is well known for its first class energy work.  </strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Last year, Arntzen de Besche represented Tullow Oil in the acquisition of spring Energy Norway AS. The firm provided mediation services in Sudan for two years with an agreement that came in place by September 2012.</p>
