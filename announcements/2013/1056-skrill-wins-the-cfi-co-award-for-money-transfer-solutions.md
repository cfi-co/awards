# Skrill wins the CFI.co Award for Money Transfer Solutions

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `1056-skrill-wins-the-cfi-co-award-for-money-transfer-solutions.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1057" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/12/Skrill.jpg" alt="Skrill" width="382" height="132" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Established twelve years ago, Skrill with its 36 million customers in 200 countries, is one of the world’s leading digital payment resources. This fast growing technology company’s objective is to become the first choice for online payments.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co Judging Panel was impressed by Skrill’s response to the World Bank’s call to, ‘Step Up and Enable the Masses.’ According to the judges, ‘Skrill is helping bring down the cost of the financial transfers of the world’s 250 million migrant workers with technology they can trust. Skrill also offers efficient and economical services to corporate clients’.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Panel is delighted to present the award to Skrill for ‘Best Secure Money Transfers, Global, 2013.’</p>
