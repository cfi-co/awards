# Best Law Firm Georgia Award for 2013 goes to DLA Piper

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `557-best-law-firm-georgia-award-for-2013-goes-to-dla-piper.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong><img class="  wp-image-558 aligncenter" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/DLA-Piper.jpg" alt="DLA Piper" width="130" height="93" /></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>DLA Piper has been named ‘Best Law Firm, Georgia, 2013’ by CFI.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Founded as a local firm, it is now uniquely well positioned to provide international expertise as well as demonstrate in- depth understanding of the jurisdiction. The majority of the firm’s work is for international clients and sophisticated Georgian businesses. Training of lawyers is a continuous process at the heart of the success of this outstanding Georgian legal firm.</p>
