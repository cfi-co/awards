# Bolsa de Valores de Colombia: Best Stock Exchange, Latin America

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `783-bolsa-de-valores-de-colombia-best-stock-exchange-latin-america.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-9075" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/33-349x218_c.jpg" alt="BVC" width="349" height="218" /></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The Bolsa de Valores de Colombia (BVC) – a private organisation – was established in 2001 with the merger of three regional exchanges in the South American country. The CFI.co Judging Panel is satisfied that BVC has now become a truly world-class exchange with a quality management system that allows for optimal client satisfaction.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The BVC has made it a priority to provide a range of simple, standardized and efficient processes running on a technical platform that is safe, trustworthy and customer-friendly. The exchange’s authorities are on the constant lookout for new and innovative market developments that answer to their ambition to offer customers an ever increasing choice of continuously improving products.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The BVC doesn´t just aspire to be the leading national exchange but a regional one as well. The judging panel considers BVC (a listed company) to be a shining example, and indeed a model, for Latin America and hence a worthy winner of its 2013 award. BVC is seen to be actively contributing to the sustainable economic growth of Colombia and the wider region.</p>
