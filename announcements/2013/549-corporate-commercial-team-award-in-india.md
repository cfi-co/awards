# Corporate & Commercial Team Award in India

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `549-corporate-commercial-team-award-in-india.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1849" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/dd1.jpg" alt="dd" width="221" height="44" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Desai &amp; Diwanji has been named ‘Best Corporate &amp; Commercial Team, India, 2013’ in the CFI Legal Awards programme.</strong> This substantial firm has three offices and a team of 185 lawyers with around half of personnel working in the corporate group. The volume of corporate deals places the firm at the very top table of Indian law firms. Growth has been more than satisfactory and the firm is well placed to take advantage of any upturn in client business optimism. Desai &amp; Diwanji acts as non-exclusive attorneys to some 150 of the biggest Indian companies.</p>
