# Charles Russell: Best Corporate & Commercial Team, Bahrain, 2013

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `516-charles-russell-best-corporate-commercial-team-bahrain-2013.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong><img class=" size-full wp-image-517 aligncenter" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/Charles_Russell_LLP_logo.jpg" alt="Charles_Russell_LLP_logo" width="170" height="73" /></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Charles Russell LLP has done good work for Edamah (Bahrain Real Estate Investment Company), was instructed by the Central Bank of Bahrain in 2009 and has a reputation for building good long term relationships with clients.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Charles Russell’s head office is in London, a further international office is located in Geneva and the Middle East office is housed at the Bahrain World Trade Centre. The judging panel commented on the Bahrain winner’s core strengths in communications, energy and property matters.</p>
