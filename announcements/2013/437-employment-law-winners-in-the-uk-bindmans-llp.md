# Employment Law Winners in the UK: Bindmans LLP

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `437-employment-law-winners-in-the-uk-bindmans-llp.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong><img class="aligncenter  wp-image-439" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/04/bindmans.jpg" alt="bindmans" width="150" height="165" /></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>According to the CFI.co judging panel, Bindmans are the rising stars of employment law in the United Kingdom and the winners of the award for 2013.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The firm, founded by Sir Geoffrey Bindman QC, is high profile with focused and intelligent employment lawyers who are making a difference and bringing in good settlements. Bindmans recently won a substantial award for an Asperger syndrome sufferer in dispute with his former law firm employer and is acting for a lap dancer who used to work at the London club Stringfellows.</p>
