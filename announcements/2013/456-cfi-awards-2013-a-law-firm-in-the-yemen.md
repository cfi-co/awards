# CFI Awards 2013: A Law Firm in the Yemen

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `456-cfi-awards-2013-a-law-firm-in-the-yemen.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-458" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/04/lsta.gif" alt="lsta" width="560" height="57" /></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The Law Offices of Sheikh Tariq Abdullah advise a number of international law firms promptly and efficiently in what is a rather difficult jurisdiction.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This firm is frequently consulted by the World Bank and has a good reputation for the quality of its services and the care and attention it lavishes on enquiries that may not be particularly profitable. The judges commented on an attitude at the firm that not all rewards for services are financial. Pro bono work at this office includes advice to the UK Seafarers Association.</p>
