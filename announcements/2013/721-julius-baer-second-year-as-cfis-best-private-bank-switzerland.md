# Julius Baer: Second Year as CFI’s Best Private Bank, Switzerland

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `721-julius-baer-second-year-as-cfis-best-private-bank-switzerland.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/jb.png"><img class="aligncenter  wp-image-1496" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2015/01/jb.png" alt="jb" width="428" height="106" /></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>With strong asset growth, effective cost control and good profitability in 2012, Julius Baer wins the CFI Private Bank, Switzerland, award for the second year running.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The judging panel commented on the benefits to the Bank following its acquisition of Merrill Lynch’s international wealth management business (outside the United States). The judges were pleased to see the continued success of a solid private bank in a competitive market.</p>
