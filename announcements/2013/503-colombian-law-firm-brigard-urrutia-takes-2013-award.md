# Colombian Law Firm Brigard & Urrutia Takes 2013 Award

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `503-colombian-law-firm-brigard-urrutia-takes-2013-award.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-504" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/Brigard-Urratia.jpg" alt="Brigard &amp; Urratia" width="160" height="160" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The long and well established Dispute Resolution Team at Brigard &amp; Urrutia is favourably placed to provide advice on a wide range of cases in Colombia.</strong> According to the CFI judging panel the firm has completed substantial and impressive work on disputes over public sector concessions, has very strong litigation skills and is very active throughout the sectors. The panel had no hesitation in naming Brigard &amp; Urrutia as ‘Best Dispute Resolution Team, Colombia’.</p>
