# Armani Hotels & Resorts: Best Luxury Hotel Experience, Global

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `1075-armani-hotels-resorts-best-luxury-hotel-experience-global-2013.json`.

[caption id="attachment_1086" align="aligncenter" width="595"]<img class="size-full wp-image-1086" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/ArmaniHotelsAwardsDubai3.jpg" alt="CFI.co award received in Dubai, UAE" width="595" height="519" /> <strong>CFI.co award received in Dubai, UAE</strong>[/caption]
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Our customer satisfaction award for hospitality in 2013 goes to Armani Hotels &amp; Resorts.</strong> Giorgio Armani says that his named properties will, ‘Offer the same kind of welcome to guests as I would privately extend to my friends and family’.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co Judging Panel was impressed – but not surprised – by the confidence of this brand. What has Armani learned during the hotel development process? Simply that, ‘Nothing is impossible’. Armani Hotels &amp; Resorts management tells us that their guests are impressed by the real haute couture hospitality on offer.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The first property was built in Dubai, at the world’s tallest building, in partnership with Emaar, UAE. According to Anthony Saccon, Head of Hotels, ‘Two icons came together, made a promise and delivered’. Enough said.</p>
