# Second Year of Success: Corporate Leadership and CSR Awards Go to Emirates

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `1111-second-year-of-success-corporate-leadership-and-csr-awards-go-to-emirates.json`.

[caption id="attachment_1916" align="aligncenter" width="349"]<img class="wp-image-1916 size-full" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/11/e2.jpg" alt="Mohammed A Al Khaja, Vice Chairman of The Emirates Airline Foundation receiving recognition from CFI.co (Marten Mark, left, and Tor Svensson, right)." width="349" height="218" /> Mohammed A Al Khaja, Vice Chairman of The Emirates Airline Foundation receiving recognition from CFI.co (Marten Mark, left, and Tor Svensson, right).[/caption]
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The corporate leadership of Emirates has been recognised by the CFI.co Judging Panel for a second consecutive year. </strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">One of the largest employers in the UAE, with a young and eco-efficient fleet, Emirates takes good care of its staff and is ever mindful of its CSR responsibilities.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co Judging Panel also commended Emirates for its ‘Outstanding Contribution to Airline CSR, Global, 2013’.</p>
