# Burgan Bank Wins CFI Award for Private Banking

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `879-burgan-bank-wins-cfi-award-for-private-banking.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-248" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/burgan-bank.jpg" alt="burgan-bank" width="325" height="80" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The CFI Judging Panel confirms Burgan Bank as ‘Best Private Bank, Kuwait, 2013’.</strong> This is a second year win for a bank which, according to the panel, is accelerating ahead of the field magnificently. Financial indicators are good, the balance sheet is solid, asset quality is high and, in 2013, share price performance markedly outshone peer banks.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Panel comments that, ‘Burgan understands and responds well to customer needs and is a strong, well trusted and innovative financial institution that is clearly on the move – and moving in the right direction.  We had no hesitation whatsoever in naming the Bank winner of this award for the second consecutive year.’</p>
