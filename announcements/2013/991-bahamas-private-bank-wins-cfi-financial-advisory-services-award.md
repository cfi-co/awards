# Bahamas Private Bank Wins CFI Financial Advisory Services Award

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `991-bahamas-private-bank-wins-cfi-financial-advisory-services-award.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-992" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/12/bsi.jpg" alt="bsi" width="164" height="84" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>BSI Overseas (Bahamas) Ltd is a wholly owned subsidiary of one of Switzerland’s oldest banks and part of the giant Generali Group.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This traditional private bank sets out to protect and increase client wealth and does so by providing the highest quality financial advisory services.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">According to the CFI Judging Panel, ‘The importance of implementing a strong corporate governance policy is well understood at BSI where there is a clear and unwavering focus on considering the client’s best interests first. The management philosophy is to provide cost effective advice and stay firmly on the client’s side at all times’.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Panel has no hesitation in confirming this Bahamas bank for the award ‘Best Financial Advisory Services, Bahamas, 2013’.</p>
