# NAB is Named Best Private Bank Australia, 2013

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `1019-nab-is-named-best-private-bank-australia-2013.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1020" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/12/nab2.jpg" alt="nab2" width="583" height="86" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>National Australia Bank is one of the largest financial institutions in the country and among the top twenty world banks by market capitalisation.</strong> The bank was founded in the late nineteenth century. Its proud heritage and the considerable strengths developed over the years plus the high quality of present management offer a compelling opportunity to private banking clients. NAB is a named Best Private Bank, Australia in the 2013 Banking Awards programme by the CFI.co Judging Panel.</p>
