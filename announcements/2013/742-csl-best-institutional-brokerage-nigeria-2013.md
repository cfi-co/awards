# CSL: Best Institutional Brokerage, Nigeria, 2013

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `742-csl-best-institutional-brokerage-nigeria-2013.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong><img class="alignright  wp-image-743" alt="csls--logo-small" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/csls-logo-small.jpg" width="147" height="163" />Institutional interest in the Nigerian markets has been growing rapidly as Nigeria moves from being a frontier to an emerging market.</strong> Levels of transparency and governance are continuing to improve both at government and corporate levels. To attract the levels of international institutional investment the Nigerian market merits requires more than just the right economic environment. To help bring international investment to Nigeria there is a need for brokers that truly understand the needs of international investors. The CFI.co judging panel are delighted to announce CSL Stockbrokers Ltd as the Best Institutional Brokerage, Nigeria 2013. Through a highly effective combination of local and international experience CSL has built a team and a service that is highly regarded by domestic and international institutional investors. CSL have an excellent track record with both liquid and illiquid assets. The judging panel were particularly impressed with the feedback provided by CSL’s seasoned team of traders providing a highly dynamic flow of information that has been producing very positive results. With regulated offices in London and New York, CSL are providing the level of access to Nigeria that the market warrants.</p>
