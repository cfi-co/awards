# Lavrynovych Wins CFI 2013 Legal Award, Ukraine

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>According to the CFI Judging Panel, the Ukraine firm Lavrynovych &amp; Partners delivers a comprehensive range of legal services to international as well as domestic companies.</strong> Client confidence and satisfaction is certainly very strong, there is constant focus on international best practice and this firm is well thought of by the international ranking agencies. The year 2013 has been a good one for Lavrynovych and the most successful in a proud nine year history of service. The Panel is delighted to confirm the award ‘Best Corporate &amp; Commercial Team, Ukraine, 2013’.</p>
