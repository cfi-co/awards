# Zimbabwe Research Team IH Securities Wins CFI’s Southern Africa Award for 2013

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `789-zimbabwe-research-team-ih-securities-wins-cfis-southern-africa-award-for-2013.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-791" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/IH-Securities.jpg" alt="IH Securities" width="241" height="107" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The CFI Judging Panel is delighted that IH Securities, Zimbabwe, has been dedicated to the sort of key, client- focused and world-class services that justify a pan Southern Africa award in the 2013 Exchanges and Broker Awards Programme.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The firm’s equity research team includes outstandingly talented individuals. As a result, the quality of work carried out is consistently very high. It is also noted that IH Securities ensures a strong post-trade experience for its clients and boasts a highly efficient back office operation. The panel has named IH Securities as possessing the Best Investment Research Team, Southern Africa.</p>
