# Dateline Energy: Moving Forward Confidently in Nigeria

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `657-dateline-energy-moving-forward-confidently-in-nigeria.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-658" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/dateline-LOGO.jpg" alt="dateline LOGO" width="480" height="232" />

<strong>The CFI judging panel congratulates Dateline Energy Services on its recent restructuring and the creation of new platforms to ensure competitiveness in a tough market place.</strong> Dateline has a good record of working for a major international player having created a scope of work that is resulting in significant new business. This company is well placed to achieve significantly improved results for its stakeholders. Dateline wins our award for ‘Best Oil &amp; Gas Project Support Services, Nigeria, 2013.’
