# Global Award for SunTec: Best Banking Systems, Services & Solutions Provider

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `806-global-award-for-suntec-best-banking-systems-services-solutions-provider.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-807" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/logo2.jpg" alt="logo" width="138" height="54" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>A value proposition without equal: The SunTec success formula is both straightforward and simple. It is also what swayed the CFI.co Judging Panel. SunTec services the global banking industry and has become a world leader in providing bespoke and compelling solutions.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Client endorsements and CFI.co voter responses suggest that SunTec’s capacity to provide the right products and services is exemplary. The company is constantly surging ahead of its competition. SunTec claims that it offers industry best practices that can help streamline client business processes. This is far from a boastful assertion since SunTec time-and-again has offered solid proof of its prowess, both in product development and business management. The CFI.co judges are therefore pleased to confirm SunTec’s global award of Best Banking Systems, Services and Solutions Provider, 2013.</p>
