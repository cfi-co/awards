# Greenwich Trust: Best Investment Bank, Nigeria

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `336-greenwich-trust-best-investment-bank-nigeria.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong><img class=" size-full wp-image-337 aligncenter" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/greenwichLogo.png" alt="" width="234" height="69" /></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Good investment banking is a bedrock for sustainable growth and to achieve this banks should balance the needs of all stakeholders.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">CFI’s awards panel are pleased to have identified Greenwich Trust Limited as a bank that does so consistently. The panel also felt that the long term financial success of Greenwich is due in large part to the excellent value set that the bank applies not only to to its investments but also to its own internal management. It is only when management teams truly live up to their mission statements that real success can be achieved. Greenwich Trust is of sufficient size to offer excellent research and strong local knowledge to help its clients and stakeholders whilst maintaining a high degree of flexibility.  The panel were delighted to announce Greenwich Trust as the Best Investment Bank, Nigeria, 2012</p>
