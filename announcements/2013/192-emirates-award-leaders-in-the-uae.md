# New Leaders in the UAE: Award for Emirates

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `192-emirates-award-leaders-in-the-uae.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1863" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2013/04/e1.jpg" alt="e1" width="151" height="151" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The airline is now the largest employer in the UAE and shows a high level of concern for the welfare of its 62,000 workers – many of whom are far away from their homelands. </strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Emirates fleet is young and eco-efficient and the airline has done much to support the Dubai Desert Conservation Reserve and eco-tourism projects in the UAE and Australia. The judging panel felt that Emirates take their CSR responsibilities very seriously and are making good progress.</p>
