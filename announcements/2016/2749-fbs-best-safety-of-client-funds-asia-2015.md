# FBS: Best Safety of Client Funds Asia 2015

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `2749-fbs-best-safety-of-client-funds-asia-2015.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter wp-image-2750" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/fbs.jpg" alt="fbs" width="207" height="207" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Honored by over 30 awards, <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="http://www.fbs.com/?utm_source=cfi&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_content=safety_award&amp;utm_campaign=en_awards">Forex company FBS</a></span> has picked up another title - “Best Safety of Client Funds Asia 2015” according to the CFI.co judging panel.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">FBS is a successful and ever-improving broker, being in business since 2009 and showing impressive results year after year around the world. FBS company employs the most advanced technologies and provides its clients with beneficial services. FBS has made remarkable progress in many regions and, according to CFI.co it has reached particular success in the Asia. FBS has perfect risk-management that allowed the company to stay still during the massive Forex collapse in January of 2015 when the Swiss National Bank pulled the plug on the Franc/Euro peg and a very impressive <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="http://www.fbs.com/promo/insuranceAgents?utm_source=cfi&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_content=safety_award&amp;utm_campaign=en_awards">Deposit Insurance program</a></span>.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">CEO of company FBS has replied to the award: “Company FBS has been working and evolving on the international market for the past 7 years. During this time we have been and will continue to provide <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="http://www.fbs.com/?utm_source=cfi&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_content=safety_award&amp;utm_campaign=en_awards">services of the highest level</a></span>, presenting our clients with the ability for capital growth at all times.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Only genuine leaders receive continuous world-wide recognition, and we are proud to have reached such heights. We would like to thank CFI.co for awarding us with the prestigious accolade “Best Safety of Client Funds Asia 2015”.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Everyday FBS continues to develop and is always in step with the current times, to guarantee clients the best quality of services, reliability and individual approach. Thanks to FBS hundreds of thousands of traders and partners alike, have gained independence and prosperity. This is largely in virtue of their aim for perfection in services.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Amidst the company services that administer clients with trust, special attention should be paid to “<span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="http://www.fbs.com/promo/insuranceAgents?utm_source=cfi&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_content=safety_award&amp;utm_campaign=en_awards">Deposit Insurance</a></span>” – this program is always popular, due to the fact that it provides an opportunity to insure ones deposit from losses. In an unforeseen situation on the market a trader may receive part or entire compensation for their investment.  Insurance is precisely why FBS fully complies with its new title “Best Safety of Client Funds Asia 2015”.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">FBS is becoming more recognized and favored not only in Asia, but also around the whole world! The Asian region has been a priority for the company for many years, and now strong growth is taking place in the Arabic world as well as some countries of the West. Every year there are openings of new <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="http://www.fbs.com/about?utm_source=cfi&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_content=safety_award&amp;utm_campaign=en_awards#contacts">regional offices</a></span>; in 2015, FBS opened representative offices in Thailand, Russia, Malaysia, Egypt and Tunisia.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">New opportunities are constantly emerging, some of which include; client <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="https://fbs.com/promo/rebate?utm_source=cfi&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_content=safety_award&amp;utm_campaign=en_awards">rebate of commission on spread</a></span>, <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="https://fbs.com/affiliate?utm_source=cfi&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_content=safety_award&amp;utm_campaign=en_awards#bounty-hunting">monthly premiums</a></span> for partners, <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="https://fbs.com/trading?utm_source=cfi&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_content=safety_award&amp;utm_campaign=en_awards#vip">VIP-program</a></span>, <span style="text-decoration: underline;"><a href="https://fbs.com/promo/lotOfApples?utm_source=cfi&amp;utm_medium=referral&amp;utm_content=safety_award&amp;utm_campaign=en_awards">the best gadgets</a></span> as presents and many more.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Company FBS – prospective broker with more than 30 awards, and worthy of a new addition “Best Safety of Client Funds Asia 2015”.</p>
