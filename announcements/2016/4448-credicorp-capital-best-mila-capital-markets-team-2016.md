# Credicorp Capital: Best MILA Capital Markets Team 2016

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `4448-credicorp-capital-best-mila-capital-markets-team-2016.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-4449" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/CredicorpCapital.jpg" alt="" width="400" height="150" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>One of the top firms in equity research, Credicorp Capital tracks the performance of regional corporates to find exceptional value for its clients. The company maintains specialised teams that monitor and investigate equities, fixed income instruments and macroeconomic developments throughout the buoyant region.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Credicorp Capital offers a comprehensive array of services available to both issuers and investors. The firm takes the long view and strives to build lasting relationships with its clients as trusted advisors who can be counted upon to offer innovative and tailor-made solutions to any given challenge. Credicorp has offices in Lima, Santiago de Chile, and Bogotá. The firm is a major player in the Mercado Integrado Latinoamericano (MILA) which integrates the capital markets of Mexico, Peru, Colombia and Chile.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">MILA offers both investors and issuers a single point of entry via any of its associated bourses to securities traded throughout the region. The participating exchanges offer brokers a unified trading platform that unlocks the entire region.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Client-focused, professional and proactive, Credicorp Capital is dedicated to excellence in the delivery of both service and research. Its analytical reports have become mandatory reading for professionals looking beyond the headlines to find out how markets are moving.
Last year, the CFI.co judging panel recognised Credicorp Capital as a MILA trendsetter and top broker. The judges again wish to congratulate Credicorp Capital for its peerless performance by naming the firm winner of the 2016 Best MILA Capital Markets Team Award.</p>
