# Atrevida Partners: Best Multi-Strategy Offshore Fund US 2016

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `3251-atrevida-partners-best-multi-strategy-offshore-fund-us-2016.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="size-full wp-image-3252 aligncenter" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/Atrevida.jpg" alt="Atrevida" width="362" height="71" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Based in New York, Atrevida Partners is an asset management firm focusing on alternative investment strategies directed away from traditional asset classes.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Serving high-net-worth-individuals and clients ranging from family offices to endowments, the company’s size allows it to take a more opportunistic stance by investing in lesser-known territories and niche-orientated funds. Thanks to the firm’s highly skilled risk management experts, these funds provide clients with better returns than traditional investment strategies, such as broad equity or fixed-income indices.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Atrevida’s Multi-Strategy Offshore Fund is a wonderful example of the company’s adeptness in this field. With over $116m in assets under management in this strategy, the fund enjoyed a 44.1% return on investment since its inception in 2007. The fund has consistently outperformed its risk-adjusted benchmarks, using the skills of its management team, a range of investment tools, and a series of successful strategies.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Not only does this fund of funds (FOF) invest in hedge funds, but also in a broad variety of financial instruments in order to profit from new opportunities, hedge risks, and maintain a varied portfolio; maximising profitability at every corner. This approach allows the firm to react quickly to global markets in order to flexibly rebalance its portfolio much faster than traditional FOFs.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Few hedge fund managers can successfully balance such a broad array of strategies and territories in order to consistently achieve such impressive risk-adjusted returns. The CFI.co judging panel declares Atrevida Partners winner of the 2016 Best Multi-Strategy Offshore Fund US Award.</p>
