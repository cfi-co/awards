# Good Monday: Best Digital Office Management Solutions Denmark 2019

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `6602-good-monday-best-digital-office-management-solutions-denmark-2019.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-6603" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/GoodMonday.jpg" alt="" width="400" height="400" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Easy like Monday morning — that’s the promise founders Mik Strøyberg and Morten Meisner-Jensen extend to the corporate clients of the Danish start-up Good Monday. The company aims to increase office efficiency through its digital management platform, connecting businesses with a network of partners and service-providers that streamline office operations and pamper employees. Good Monday offers companies scalable packages, with one centralized payment system for various weekly/monthly products and services. The packages range from weekly cleaning services and snack deliveries for small offices to yoga classes and breakfast buffets for larger enterprises. There’s a plan to suit any budget, and Good Monday welcomes the opportunity to tailor solutions to a client’s needs. After accepting the agreement, clients deal directly with Good Monday, while it leverages its extensive network to source anything from office supplies and party favours to team-building activities and employee recognition awards. Good Monday presents clients with a comprehensive overview of office expenditures and service agreements, and assigns a personal concierge to assist with seamless, trouble-free operation. The corporate culture at Good Monday, from founders to service providers, is grounded in respect for the client and pride in the work. The CFI.co judging panel endorses the belief that healthy work environments foster engaged and productive employees. The judges also acknowledge that Good Monday has proven itself a capable and creative partner, facilitating smoothly functioning offices and allowing clients to focus on managing their businesses. Good Monday is the winner of the 2019 award for Best Digital Office Management Solutions (Denmark).</p>
