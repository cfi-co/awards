# C&R Group: Best Share Registry Services East Africa 2026

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13563-cr-group-best-share-registry-services-east-africa-2026.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-11913" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/C-and-R-Group-jpg.webp" alt="C and R Group" width="500" height="398" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">C&amp;R Group merits recognition for disciplined execution in a subdued capital-markets cycle, demonstrating that service excellence is measured by reliability, control and stakeholder confidence rather than transaction volume. The company's strategic clarity is evident in its deliberate shift from event-led registry work to scalable digital participation infrastructure, widening its addressable market beyond listed issuers to membership-based organisations and thereby reducing cyclicality risk. Judging emphasis falls on governance and assurance: by embedding regulatory literacy into client outcomes, C&amp;R Group strengthens market integrity while protecting issuers from avoidable compliance failures. Its convening role in 2025 governance conferences, delivered with regulators including the Unclaimed Financial Assets Authority and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, shows leadership that is practical and preventative, translating complex obligations into operational readiness and audit-quality reporting. The panel also notes mature risk management in data stewardship, with training that links policy, human behaviour and emerging AI-enabled threats, reinforcing resilience where registry providers are systemically exposed. Cross-border execution further differentiates the entry: expansion in Uganda, including onboarding a banking client, indicates replicable processes and credible controls across jurisdictions, supported by consistent service-level discipline. Finally, C&amp;R Group's handling of demanding corporate actions, including a squeeze-out progressing to a 100 percent compulsory takeover, evidences procedural rigour, transparent communications and dependable technology under pressure. The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging Panel congratulates C&amp;R Group on winning the Best Share Registry Services 2026 Award (East Africa).</p>
