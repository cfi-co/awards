# IrisGuard: Excellence in Biometric Identity Payment Solutions Global 2025

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13336-irisguard-excellence-in-biometric-identity-payment-solutions-global-2025.json`.

&nbsp;

<img class="aligncenter  wp-image-13337" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Irisguard.jpg" alt="Irisguard" width="335" height="255" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">IrisGuard merits recognition for translating high-assurance iris biometrics into payment and entitlement outcomes that are operationally proven, ethically framed, and measurably material at scale. The company's distinguishing strength is not novelty alone, but disciplined execution of one-to-many identification in environments where cards, PINs, documents, and connectivity are unreliable. Its platform design shows strategic clarity: integrates with existing rails (ATMs, post offices, mobile cash-out agents, supermarkets and wallets) while keeping the beneficiary experience simple, contact-free and fast. Impact is evidenced by throughput and reach: IrisGuard reports processing over $1.3bn of aid assistance per year and serving over 5.8 million beneficiaries, demonstrating sustained delivery rather than pilot-stage promise. Governance and risk management are treated as core product features, with ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 27001:2013 certifications reinforcing quality discipline and information security. Innovation is validated externally through the 2021 GSMA GLOMO Award for their mobile iris payment solution. The company's UK headquarters in Milton Keynes, coupled with regional delivery capability, in Amman Jordan underpins resilience and responsiveness across multi-stakeholder deployments. The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging Panel congratulates IrisGuard on winning the Excellence in Biometric Identity Payment Solutions Global 2025 Award.</p>
