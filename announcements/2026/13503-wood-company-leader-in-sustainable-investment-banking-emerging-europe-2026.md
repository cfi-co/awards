# Wood & Company: Leader in Sustainable Investment Banking Emerging Europe 2026

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13503-wood-company-leader-in-sustainable-investment-banking-emerging-europe-2026.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-13504" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Wood-and-Co.jpg" alt="Wood and Company " width="600" height="249" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>A 42 MWp solar power plant in Romania and a 70 MW / 160 MWh battery storage system in Sweden give Wood &amp; Company's Renewables Sub-Fund asset-level substance in a market where sustainable finance depends on demonstrable delivery. Finnish battery projects under construction add to that platform, linking energy-transition exposure with return discipline rather than treating ESG as a separate label.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Regional reach is material. Wood &amp; Company manages € 5.8bn in assets, covers more than 150  traded companies and operates across 22  capital markets, giving it scale across investment banking, asset management and advisory work in Central and Eastern Europe.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The most persuasive strategic feature is integration. Sustainability considerations appear in M&amp;A advice, infrastructure mandates, real estate funds and investor education, with clients encouraged to assess how much of their portfolio is green or ESG-certified. This aligns with CFI.co's preference for strategies that connect purpose, control and commercial resilience.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Building-level execution provides clear evidence of control. Across its real estate portfolio, the firm measures energy efficiency, installs photovoltaic capacity, monitors waste separation and uses CREM modelling for individual assets and portfolios.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Governance capacity reaches beyond product design. Wood &amp; Company seeded Esgrovia , an ESG and energy-certification business serving the Czech and Slovak markets, and has prepared a green bond framework. That matters where green bond issuance remains comparatively modest.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Inclusive employment practices reinforce stewardship culture. With more than 500 employees, 13 nationalities and structured part-time opportunities for women returning from maternity leave, the firm presents sustainability as an operating discipline as well as an investment theme. The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging Panel congratulates Wood &amp; Company on winning the Leader in Sustainable Investment Banking Emerging Europe 2026 award.</p>
