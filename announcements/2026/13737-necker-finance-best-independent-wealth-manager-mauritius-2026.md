# Necker Finance: Best Independent Wealth Manager Mauritius 2026

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13737-necker-finance-best-independent-wealth-manager-mauritius-2026.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-13735" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/NeckerFinance-1.jpg" alt="Necker Finance" width="500" height="88" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Established in Mauritius in 2014, Necker Finance is an independent wealth management firm regulated by the Financial Services Commission (FSC), holding Investment Adviser (Unrestricted) and Distribution of Financial Products licences.</strong> The firm provides discretionary portfolio management and investment advisory services tailored to each client's financial objectives, investment horizon, liquidity requirements, currency exposure and risk tolerance.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">At the heart of Necker Finance's approach is a clear separation between investment management and asset custody. Client assets are held in their own names with independent custodian banks, selected according to their financial strength, service capabilities and suitability. This structure promotes transparency, enhances investor protection and provides clients with flexibility in choosing their banking partners.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Necker Finance operates through an open-architecture investment model, offering access to a broad range of asset classes, currencies, investment solutions and internationally recognised asset managers. Its investment philosophy emphasises capital preservation, disciplined risk management and long-term wealth creation. Portfolios are actively monitored and adjusted to changing market conditions, with particular attention to diversification, income generation, interest-rate sensitivity and downside risk.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Necker Finance Mauritius also benefits from valuable synergies with its Geneva-based affiliate, combining complementary investment expertise, international market insights and an extensive network of financial partners. While each entity operates under its own distinct regulatory framework, this collaboration strengthens the firm's investment capabilities and international perspective, particularly for entrepreneurs, families and internationally mobile clients.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Its independent structure, direct access to senior investment professionals and close relationships with leading financial institutions enable Necker Finance to deliver personalised solutions and build enduring client relationships.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Beyond investment management, Necker Finance adopts a comprehensive wealth management approach, recognising that investment decisions are closely connected to tax considerations, succession planning and family circumstances. Working alongside independent legal, tax and estate-planning specialists, the firm helps clients coordinate their wealth strategies across jurisdictions while respecting applicable regulatory requirements.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The firm continues to develop its digital capabilities in portfolio reporting, risk monitoring and secure client communication, while maintaining the importance of human expertise, accessibility and individual judgement. Technology supports the investment process, but personal relationships and a deep understanding of each client's circumstances remain central to its philosophy.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Through its combination of independent advice, open architecture, disciplined portfolio management, transparent custody arrangements and international expertise, Necker Finance demonstrates a strong commitment to protecting and growing client wealth over the long term.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging Panel congratulates Necker Finance on winning the Best Independent Wealth Manager 2026 Award (Mauritius).</p>
