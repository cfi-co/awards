<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>CRC Credit Bureau Ltd merits recognition for disciplined execution in a market where credit infrastructure is both systemically important and operationally demanding.</strong> The company demonstrates strategic clarity by investing in capabilities that strengthen lender decisioning and consumer trust simultaneously, rather than pursuing growth at the expense of data integrity. Its published performance indicators point to scale and reliability: 180m+ credit reports generated, 2,000+ member institutions served, and a stated 99.9 percent data accuracy rate, evidencing mature governance over data quality and controls. Product strategy shows purposeful differentiation through solutions that integrate credit information with scoring and monitoring, including consumer-facing subscriptions that incorporate a CRC FICO Score and structured monthly alerting, aligning incentives towards responsible borrowing and early risk detection. Execution quality is reinforced by visible improvements to digital access and security posture, with recent market reporting highlighting an enhanced mobile app featuring biometric login and encryption, signalling a pragmatic approach to usability without compromising confidentiality. Importantly, CRC's leadership has sustained external validation over multiple cycles, with its own disclosures and independent reporting citing consecutive CFI.co recognition through 2025 as Best Credit Bureau, indicating consistency rather than one-off performance. In aggregate, the company's resilience, measurable operational outcomes, and credible innovation pipeline justify panel confidence in its 2026 award, underpinned by clear accountability and responsive delivery. The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging Panel congratulates CRC Credit Bureau Ltd on winning the Best Credit Bureau 2026 Award (Nigeria).</p>
