# Containers Printers: Most Innovative Packaging Technology Global 2026

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13671-containers-printers-most-innovative-packaging-technology-global-2026.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-10680" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Containers-Printers-new-logo.jpg" alt="Containers-Printers-new-logo" width="400" height="70" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>At a time when packaging clients are delaying sustainability investments yet facing tighter authentication, traceability and reporting obligations, the company's value lies in readiness rather than rhetoric.</strong> Founded in 1981, it has evolved from metal-container printing into a dual platform spanning metal packaging, flexible laminates, digital protection and sustainable packaging services for customers in more than 30 countries. The panel views this breadth as strategically important: innovation is embedded in practical customer problems, including counterfeit risk, grey-market diversion, supply-chain visibility, consumer engagement, lifecycle assessment, Extended Producer Responsibility reporting, Singapore Mandatory Packaging Reporting and UK Plastic Packaging Tax compliance. Containers Printers combines visible and invisible protection technologies with digital integration, while its sustainability offer addresses material reduction, recycled content, recyclability, compostability and carbon-footprint measurement. Equally persuasive is its disciplined execution under difficult market conditions. Rather than forcing uncommercial solutions into hesitant markets, management has built data capability, preserved optionality and prioritised automation, productivity and Kaizen-led operational improvement. Hundreds of improvement projects recently indicate a culture that converts technical ambition into repeatable manufacturing gains. Its two Singapore sites, sector exposure across nutrition, food and medical packaging, and recognition through two Sustainable Packaging Awards in 2020 demonstrate both resilience and external validation. The result is a technology model that is customer-relevant, standards-aware and commercially patient, with potential to scale as regulation and brand accountability intensify. The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging Panel congratulates Containers Printers on winning the Most Innovative Packaging Technology 2026 Award (Global).</p>
