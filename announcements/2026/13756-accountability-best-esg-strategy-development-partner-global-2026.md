# AccountAbility: Best ESG Strategy Development Partner Global 2026

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13756-accountability-best-esg-strategy-development-partner-global-2026.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-13754" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/AccountAbility.jpg" alt="AccountAbility" width="500" height="98" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>AccountAbility combines freely available AA1000 sustainability standards with advisory delivery, linking stakeholder evidence to strategy, governance and implementation.</strong> Its work is anchored in Inclusivity, Materiality, Responsiveness and Impact, principles intended to turn sustainability priorities into accountable decisions.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The revision of the AA1000 Stakeholder Engagement Standard, first issued in 2011 and last updated in 2015, responds to expectations around co-creation, digital engagement and traceable decision-making. More than 30 interviews informed the initial drafting, followed by a public consultation involving over 40 respondents. The revised standard places impact at the centre of engagement processes.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">For a financial services client seeking a clearer sustainability baseline, an ESG health check combined document review, stakeholder discussions and peer research to produce a roadmap with short- and longer-term actions. A US real estate investment trust engaged AccountAbility to refresh a 2020 materiality assessment, using interviews and workshops to identify material topics, impacts, risks and opportunities.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Certified Stakeholder Engagement practitioner programme extends this approach beyond individual mandates. To date, the CSEP Training has qualified over 50 practitioners across utilities, hospitality, sport, assurance, and business networks. The 3-module curriculum covers engagement design, stakeholder mapping, implementation, risk management, self-evaluation and assurance.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Governance gives this operating model additional discipline. AccountAbility is a public benefit corporation, with its mission embedded in its bylaws, while its standards operate as a freely available public good and advisory work tests their application. This combination supports proportionate responses to emerging reporting and double-materiality requirements.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">AccountAbility demonstrates a considered approach to ESG strategy development, combining technical stewardship, stakeholder engagement and implementable guidance. The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging panel congratulates AccountAbility on winning the Best ESG Strategy Development Partner 2026 Award (Global)</p>
