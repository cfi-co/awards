# Bank of Industry (BOI): Excellence in Transformative Institutional Direction West Africa 2026

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13665-bank-of-industry-boi-excellence-in-transformative-institutional-direction-west-a.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-13663" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/BankOfIndustryBOI.png" alt="Bank of Industry (BOI)" width="500" height="99" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Bank of Industry (BOI) merits recognition for institutional direction that is both disciplined and explicitly developmental, translating leadership intent into measurable delivery while strengthening the organisation's operating model.</strong> Since the 2023 leadership transition, BOI has prioritised a clear governance logic: protect financial sustainability through cost rationalisation and revenue diversification, while hardwiring impact into decision-making rather than treating it as narrative. The creation of dedicated research, impact and sustainability functions evidences a deliberate shift towards data-led credit judgement, ex-ante impact screening and real-time outcome monitoring, improving accountability for development additionality. Execution quality is demonstrated by the bank's operational modernisation agenda, notably the Turn Around Time programme designed to digitise end-to-end lending workflows and materially compress approval cycles, a practical reform that improves client experience and internal control simultaneously. Strategic resilience is further reflected in BOI's ability to scale activity in a volatile macroeconomic environment: it reported record disbursements of ₦636bn to over 7,000 enterprises in 2025, signalling both balance-sheet capacity and delivery discipline. Importantly, BOI's direction shows regional consciousness without mandate drift, backing value-chain investments with ecosystem effects across Nigeria, including textiles and energy-linked industrial supply. The panel also notes credible external validation through multiple 2025 recognitions cited by BOI, indicating that reforms are visible beyond the institution, and that management has embedded a culture of continuous improvement. The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging Panel congratulates Bank of Industry (BOI) on winning the Excellence in Transformative Institutional Direction 2026 Award (West Africa).</p>
