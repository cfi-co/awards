# Acin: Best Operational Risk Management UK 2023

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `11252-acin-best-operational-risk-management-uk-2023.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-11253" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Acin-jpg.webp" alt="Acin" width="500" height="152" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Acin has established a strong track record of delivering value, as evidenced by the conﬁdence of its investor base and the repeat business of its clients.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The risk analytics and intelligence firm is supported by long-term strategic investors like Fitch, a global leader of credit ratings and capital market research, and five of the world’s largest banks: JP Morgan, Citi, BNP Paribas, Barclays and Lloyds.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Acin offers a quality assessment of a clients’ data readiness, a platform for enterprise-wide risk management and a peer network for trigger-based control of the risk framework.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Acin employs meticulous attention to detail and adaptability to help clients in financial services address regulatory and operational risks in a quantitative, measured and well-informed method. Risks abound in modern business, whether resulting from malicious intent or human error.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Acin AI-powered platform gives financial services greater control over any non-financial risks (NFR) caused by compliance failures, misconduct, technology or operational challenges.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">From its UK headquarters, Acin has created a global risk management hub and data network receiving a stream of multi-formatted updates from banks and financial firms.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Client data is authenticated and anonymised before becoming part of the Acin network, which enables peer connectivity and calibration through a dynamic risk assessment framework.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Risk intelligence and threat analysis is conducted on a real-time basis and is complemented by third-party feeds concerning losses, events, news and regulatory changes.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co judging panel announces Acin as the 2023 award winner for Best Operational Risk Management Partner (UK).</p>
