# WEALINS: Best Wealth Insurance Solutions Europe 2023

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12148-wealins-best-wealth-insurance-solutions-europe-2023.json`.

<img class="aligncenter wp-image-12146 size-full" title="WEALINS" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/WEALINS-jpg.webp" alt="WEALINS" width="500" height="323" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">WEALINS empowers clients by creating exclusive solutions to transfer their wealth. The company has been in operation over the past three decades, building a strong experience in the insurance industry. It specialises in life insurance and capitalisation solutions designed to structure and preserve clients' wealth for future generations. The <a href="https://cfi.co/countries/luxembourg/">Luxembourg</a>-based company caters to wealthy and international clientele and its solutions take into account the clients’ evolving situations. WEALINS has an active presence in nine EU countries and an in-depth knowledge across different markets. Customised solutions exist for clients residing outside of the European Economic Area. Trust is a cornerstone of WEALINS' corporate culture. It cultivates trust among clients and partners by going the extra mile to accommodate their needs. Partnerships with asset and wealth managers boost distribution, while dedicated after-sales service ensures continuous customer support. WEALINS has invested to upgrade its  IT system and entrusted management of the newly developed tech infrastructure to an external specialist. The investment in the digital platform is already paying dividends in terms of improved communications and access to information, reduced paperwork, unified processing and accurate data. The digital push is meant to complement - not overshadow - the company's human-to-human approach to client relationships. Many of WEALINS clients are entrepreneurs seeking long-term investment opportunities that promise stable and continuous growth. WEALINS enjoys low turnover within its workforce of 130 professionals. The CFl.co judging panel announces WEALINS as the 2023 award winner for Best <a href="https://wealins.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Wealth Insurance Solutions</a> (Europe).</p>
