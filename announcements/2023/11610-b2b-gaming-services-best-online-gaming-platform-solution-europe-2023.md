# B2B Gaming Services: Best Online Gaming Platform Solution Europe 2023

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `11610-b2b-gaming-services-best-online-gaming-platform-solution-europe-2023.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-6817" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/B2B.jpg" alt="B2B" width="235" height="109" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Since its launch in 1997, the operation has been pushing the boundaries of play.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The impetus was the need to establish a “future-ready” business model that would provide end-customers with an entertaining gaming experience and safeguard their interests.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The future-forward company B2B GAMING SERVICES, is driven by principles of “servant-leadership” and “agile innovation”.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">It has developed a modular and scalable platform solution for flexible and sustainable business growth to serve the needs of online and land-based betting &amp; gaming with the objective to fully host the progressive demands of the players across regulated markets.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">B2B GAMING SERVICES markets the platform as a powerhouse for possibilities.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The company foresaw a shift in the gaming landscape — with the line between business providers, aggregators, and operators blurring overtime — and it has strategically positioned itself to capitalize upon that evolution.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">B2B GAMING SERVICES operations are centered across the gaming spectrum.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The company is harnessing advances in AI to empower the player with more informed decisions, and to create more personalized user experiences.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">B2B GAMING SERVICES is licensed since 2007 by the Malta Gaming Authority and since 2012 in Greece under the then transitional regime, since 2021 has acquired Greek Permanent Licenses issued by the Hellenic Gaming Commission upon the institution of the new law in the Country of Greece.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">B2B GAMING SERVICES is granted with the full stack of accreditations and certifications, including ISO 9001: 2015 for quality &amp; ISO 27001:2013 for management systems information security on the scope of “Provision of operation systems, gambling services and online gambling, in all countries where the company is licensed (Sports betting, Live Sports betting, Livestreaming, Casino, Live Casino, Games, Virtual Sports)”.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Company has immediate expansion plans, and we expect to see their existing success replicated in new markets.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co judging panel presents <a href="https://cfi.co/menu/corporate/2023/07/online-gaming-sector-quick-to-appreciate-role-of-ai-to-reduce-risk-and-empower-growth/">B2B GAMING SERVICES</a> – a repeat program winner - with 2023 award for Best Online Gaming Platform Solution (Europe).</p>
