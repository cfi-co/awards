# Ventures Middle East: Best Strategic Business Advisory Services MENA 2022

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `11033-ventures-middle-east-best-strategic-business-advisory-services-mena-2022.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-11034" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Ventures-Middle-East-jpg.webp" alt="Ventures Middle East" width="573" height="115" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Over the past two decades, Ventures Middle East has successfully completed more than 1,000 projects and achieved a client retention rate of 92 percent. The consultancy was founded by a three-person team; now it has over 200 employees with experience and expertise covering more than 20 industries. Ventures Middle East is a client-centric company with a can-do attitude. No project is too big or too small to benefit from its research and consultancy capabilities. It has established a strong presence across the Middle East and North Africa (MENA), bringing international know-how to local markets. Ventures Middle East has attracted a multicultural workforce, representing 50 nationalities and eight languages, all united behind the company’s core values of objectivity, integrity, excellence, collaboration, innovation and resilience. Ventures Middle East helps clients achieve a competitive edge with customised analysis and strategic insights across industries and sectors. Advisory services cover the gamut of industry analysis, market reports, competitor assessments and market sizing. It provides assistance with investment management, helping clients to identify opportunities and mitigate risks. It can give businesses an overview of customers’ experience, including engagement and satisfaction levels across service suites and marketing campaigns. It can guide clients through digital transformation, set up internal business units for shared services and implement strategies for optimised operational performance and more efficient supply chains. It’s also an expert head-hunter. The CFI.co judging panel announces Ventures Middle East as the 2022 award winner for Best Strategic Business Advisory Services (MENA).</p>
