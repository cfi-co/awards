# ABC Capital: Best Stock Brokerage Advisory Team East Africa 2023

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `11847-abc-capital-best-stock-brokerage-advisory-team-east-africa-2023.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-11848" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/ABCCapital-jpg.webp" alt="ABC Capital" width="500" height="443" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">ABC Capital markets itself as a one-stop shop for financial solutions. The firm is based in Kenya and has been an active member of the Nairobi Securities Exchange for nearly seven decades. ABC Capital was acquired by the African Banking Corporation in 2008. The firm capitalises on the network of its parent company, which has 12 bank branches in Kenya and two in Uganda, as an ideal channel for customer engagement and product distribution.  ABC Capital works with clients throughout East Africa, offering access to a wide range of Nairobi Securities Exchange listed securities, portfolio management and corporate finance services. It’s proud to contribute to financial inclusion across the region. ABC Capital cultivates strong professional relationships to understand clients’ financial circumstances, investment goals and risk tolerance before making recommendations. ABC Capital serves as an agent of the Central Bank of Kenya and is a proactive player in primary and secondary bonds markets. The firm is committed to protecting clients’ interests and has assembled a multidisciplinary team to ensure services exceed expectations. Team members engage, listen and respond to clients in timely fashion. Clients benefit from the firm’s nimble turnaround and seamless execution of trades. Research analysts consistently monitor market happenings to identify opportunities and mitigate risks. ABC Capital levers a well-developed investors network to help entrepreneurs raise funding for their ventures. The CFI.co judging panel announces ABC Capital as the 2023 award winner for Best Stock Brokerage Advisory Team (East Africa).</p>
