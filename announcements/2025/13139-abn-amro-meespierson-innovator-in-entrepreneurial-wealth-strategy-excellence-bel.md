# ABN AMRO MeesPierson: Innovator in Entrepreneurial Wealth Strategy Excellence Belgium 2025

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13139-abn-amro-meespierson-innovator-in-entrepreneurial-wealth-strategy-excellence-bel.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-13141" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/ABN-AMRO.jpg" alt="ABN-AMRO" width="500" height="122" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">ABN AMRO MeesPierson demonstrates a forward-thinking and holistic approach to private banking, placing the needs of entrepreneurs at the centre of its strategy. The bank recognises that entrepreneurial wealth management extends beyond a business exit, supporting clients at every milestone of their professional and personal journeys. By integrating corporate finance services into its wealth management offering, ABN AMRO MeesPierson provides guidance on mergers, succession planning, and company transfers, while also offering operational credit solutions to support businesses during growth. This ensures a seamless service in which one dedicated relationship manager coordinates private and professional aspects, working alongside specialist teams. Sustainability has been a core priority for more than two decades, underlining the bank’s commitment to helping clients navigate environmental, social, and governance challenges. Entrepreneurs benefit from tailored advice that highlights not only risks but also opportunities arising from sustainable transformation, with insights presented in a practical manner. The partnership with the Sustainability Centre of Vlerick Business School embodies the bank’s engagement in developing and sharing knowledge practical knowledge related to sustainable transition. The bank also recognises the significance of wealth transfer from baby boomers to the next generation, collaborating with Solvay Business School to prepare future leaders through education and networking. This balance of tradition, innovation, and responsibility positions the bank as a trusted partner for entrepreneurs in Belgium. The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging Panel congratulates ABN AMRO MeesPierson on winning the 2025 Innovator in Entrepreneurial Wealth Strategy Excellence Award (Belgium).</p>
