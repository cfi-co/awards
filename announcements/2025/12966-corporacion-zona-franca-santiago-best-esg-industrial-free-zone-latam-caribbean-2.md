# Corporación Zona Franca Santiago: Best ESG Industrial Free Zone LatAm & Caribbean 2025

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12966-corporacion-zona-franca-santiago-best-esg-industrial-free-zone-latam-caribbean-2.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12970" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/CZFS.jpg" alt="CZFS" width="400" height="127" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Corporación Zona Franca Santiago has established itself as a leading force in sustainable industrial development, demonstrating a steadfast commitment to environmental, social, and governance (ESG) principles. As the largest and most dynamic free zone in the Dominican Republic, it provides a robust platform for economic growth, fostering innovation and sustainable practices across a diverse range of industries. The company has integrated cutting-edge technologies and green infrastructure to minimise environmental impact while enhancing operational efficiency. Its comprehensive social initiatives support workforce development, education, and community engagement, reinforcing its dedication to inclusive growth and shared prosperity. Governance structures are underpinned by transparency, accountability, and ethical business practices, ensuring long-term resilience, investor confidence, and regulatory compliance. Corporación Zona Franca Santiago has also positioned itself as a regional benchmark in circular economy practices, reducing waste and promoting resource efficiency within its dynamic industrial ecosystem. By fostering strong partnerships with international stakeholders, it continues to attract high-value investments, further strengthening its competitive edge in the global market. The company’s proactive approach to sustainability has been recognised through various prestigious industry accolades, underscoring its role as a model for responsible industrial free zones. Through strategic planning, continuous innovation, and unwavering commitment to ESG excellence, it continues to drive economic and environmental progress while maintaining high standards of corporate responsibility and sustainable development. CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates Corporación Zona Franca Santiago on winning the 2025 award for Best ESG Industrial Free Zone (LatAm &amp; Caribbean).</p>
