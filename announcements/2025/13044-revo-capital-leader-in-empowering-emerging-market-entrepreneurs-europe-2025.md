# Revo Capital: Leader in Empowering Emerging Market Entrepreneurs Europe 2025

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13044-revo-capital-leader-in-empowering-emerging-market-entrepreneurs-europe-2025.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-13046" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Revo.jpg" alt="Revo" width="500" height="167" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Revo Capital has emerged as a pioneering force in the European venture capital landscape, with a dedicated focus on enabling innovation across emerging markets. Founded by former telecom executives with extensive experience in product development and corporate leadership, Revo leverages its background to back early-stage technology companies that possess both local strength and global potential. Since its inception, the firm has raised and deployed three funds, including Turkey’s largest fund at its time of launch, collectively channelling capital into over 40 high-growth ventures. With an emphasis on Series A and B investments—a segment underserved in its core markets—Revo has demonstrated a remarkable ability to catalyse growth, evidenced by 17 successful exits and the nurturing of unicorns such as Getir. Its portfolio has attracted over $3.4bn in follow-on investments, affirming its strategic value as a first mover and trusted partner. The firm excels at internationalising its companies, ensuring R&amp;D remains in Turkey while relocating headquarters and revenue streams to the US, UK, or EU to access hard currency. Supported by global institutional LPs including IFC, EBRD, EIF, and DEG, Revo is also known for its proprietary growth platform that offers support in marketing, senior recruitment, international expansion, and investor relations. The firm’s annual ecosystem event, hosting stakeholders from across Europe, further underscores its commitment to shaping the regional VC landscape. CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates Revo Capital on winning the 2025 award for Leader in Empowering Emerging Market Entrepreneurs (Europe).</p>
