# GCC Board Directors Institute: Visionary in Shaping Future Board Leaders Middle East 2025

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `13071-gcc-board-directors-institute-visionary-in-shaping-future-board-leaders-middle-e.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-5327" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/BDI.jpg" alt="BDI" width="324" height="128" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">GCC Board Directors Institute (GCC BDI) plays a pivotal role in enhancing corporate governance and leadership standards across the GCC region. Established 18 years ago, it has become the only Gulf professional body recognised as part of the Global Network of Director Institutes (GNDI). Its work is anchored in local engagement, adapting best practices to the distinct regulatory and cultural contexts of each GCC country. With over 4,400 members, GCC BDI has expanded its reach, and is operating chapters in the UAE, Bahrain, and Saudi Arabia, with plans for Oman by the end of this year. The organisation’s offerings include director certification and governance training, with three certification levels for directors and one for board secretaries and facilitated by both local and international faculty across their offerings.  GCC BDI has been instrumental in shaping board effectiveness by supporting and educating directors, governance professionals, and stakeholders in the GCC. Its thought leadership initiatives reflect a commitment to diversity, with women on boards emerging as a central advocacy focus. This agenda is reinforced by a board of directors and chapter leadership composed of industry figures. With 27 employees, GCC BDI maintains impact through strategic partnerships, capacity-building, and dedication to professionalising board leadership. Its ability to balance global standards with local relevance underscores its credibility and influence. As GCC economies continue to open and compete for international capital, GCC BDI’s mission remains vital in fostering transparent, accountable, and investable ecosystems. The Capital Finance International (CFI.co) Judging Panel congratulates GCC Board Directors Institute on winning the 2025 award for Visionary in Shaping Future Board Leaders (Middle East).</p>
