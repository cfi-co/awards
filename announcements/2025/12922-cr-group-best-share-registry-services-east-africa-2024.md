# C&R Group: Best Share Registry Services East Africa 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12922-cr-group-best-share-registry-services-east-africa-2024.json`.

<img class="aligncenter  wp-image-11913" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/C-and-R-Group-jpg.webp" alt="C and R Group" width="368" height="293" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">C&amp;R Group has established itself as a leader in share registry services across East Africa, leveraging advanced technology to enhance efficiency and accessibility for clients and regulators. The company has progressively integrated process automation, predictive analytics, and interactive reporting, enabling clients to customise industry analyses and shareholder trends. Its virtual AGM system has played a pivotal role in increasing shareholder participation, particularly among mid-tier investors who previously found it challenging to attend physical meetings. C&amp;R Group continues to provide thought leadership through industry analysis and data analytics, supporting both regulatory bodies and listed companies in navigating evolving market dynamics. A key achievement in 2024 was the management of a complex, competing offer for a delisting process in Kenya, which was seamlessly executed using an electronic platform. This approach streamlined communications, reduced logistical complexities, and ensured real-time adjustments for shareholders. The firm’s commitment to innovation extends beyond technology, as it prioritises team development through training and knowledge-sharing initiatives. By maintaining a balance between institutional expertise and new talent, C&amp;R Group ensures a high standard of service delivery. Its forward-looking strategy focuses on expanding the capabilities of its core systems, exploring predictive reporting, and deepening automation processes to further enhance transparency and efficiency in share registry services. As the industry evolves, C&amp;R Group remains at the forefront, demonstrating agility, resilience, and strategic foresight. The CFI.co Judging Panel proudly congratulates C&amp;R Group on winning the 2024 award for Best Share Registry Services (East Africa).</p>
