# QNB Egypt: Best SME Bank and Best Retail Bank Egypt 2025

<p style="text-align: justify;">QNB Egypt is a leading financial institution committed to delivering innovative banking solutions tailored to individuals and businesses. With a strong focus on small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), the bank provides a comprehensive suite of financial products, including tailored lending solutions, trade finance, and cash management services. It actively supports SMEs by offering advisory services and digital banking tools that enhance operational efficiency and growth potential. In retail banking, QNB Egypt has established itself as a trusted partner by delivering a wide range of personalised financial solutions, including savings and investment products, mortgage financing, and consumer lending. Its dedication to digital transformation is evident in the continuous development of secure and user-friendly mobile and online banking platforms, ensuring seamless transactions and enhanced accessibility. The bank prioritises customer satisfaction by integrating advanced technology with personalised service, fostering financial inclusion across diverse demographics. QNB Egypt also upholds strong corporate social responsibility values, actively contributing to economic development and community well-being through financial literacy initiatives and sustainable banking practices. Its forward-thinking approach and commitment to excellence have reinforced its reputation as a reliable and progressive financial services provider. By maintaining high standards of service, innovation, and inclusivity, the bank continues to play a crucial role in strengthening Egypt’s financial landscape and empowering future generations through accessible and customer-focused banking solutions. The CFI.co Judging Panel proudly congratulates QNB Egypt on winning the 2025 awards for Best SME Bank and Best Retail Bank (Egypt).</p>
