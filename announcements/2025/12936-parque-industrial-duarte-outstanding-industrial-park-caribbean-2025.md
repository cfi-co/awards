# Parque Industrial Duarte: Outstanding Industrial Park Caribbean 2025

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12936-parque-industrial-duarte-outstanding-industrial-park-caribbean-2025.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12937" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Duarte.jpg" alt="Duarte" width="500" height="133" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Parque Industrial Duarte has emerged as a key driver of industrial development in the Caribbean, demonstrating an unwavering commitment to operational excellence, sustainability, and economic growth. Strategically located in the Dominican Republic, the park provides a fully integrated infrastructure designed to support a diverse range of industries, from manufacturing to logistics and technology. Its master-planned layout, extensive facilities, and secure environment create optimal conditions for both local and international businesses to thrive. The park boasts over 1m square metres of developed land and continues to expand, reflecting strong demand and confidence from the private sector. Emphasising environmental responsibility, Parque Industrial Duarte has incorporated sustainable practices such as solar energy integration, waste management systems, and green building initiatives. The management team prioritises efficiency, customer service, and continuous improvement, ensuring that tenant companies receive consistent support and tailored solutions. Its close proximity to major ports and transport networks reinforces its position as a regional logistics hub. Furthermore, the park contributes significantly to job creation and community development, enhancing socio-economic conditions across the region. With over 40 years of operational history, Parque Industrial Duarte has become a symbol of industrial resilience and innovation, offering a blueprint for industrial parks throughout Latin America and the Caribbean. Its ongoing investment in smart infrastructure, digital connectivity, and training programmes further cements its status as a forward-thinking and competitive industrial leader. The CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates Parque Industrial Duarte on winning the 2025 award for Outstanding Industrial Park (Caribbean).</p>
