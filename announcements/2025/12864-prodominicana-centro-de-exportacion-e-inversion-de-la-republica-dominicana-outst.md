# ProDominicana - Centro de Exportación e Inversión de la República Dominicana: Outstanding Contribution to Sustainable Economic Growth Caribbean 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12864-prodominicana-centro-de-exportacion-e-inversion-de-la-republica-dominicana-outst.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12866" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/ProDominicana.jpg" alt="ProDominicana" width="500" height="191" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">ProDominicana, the Centre for Export and Investment of the Dominican Republic, serves as a vital force driving sustainable economic development in the Caribbean. By implementing strategic initiatives, the organisation has significantly enhanced the country’s global trade position, promoting exports and attracting foreign direct investment (FDI). ProDominicana has been instrumental in facilitating partnerships between domestic enterprises and international markets, leading to economic diversification and fostering growth across key sectors such as tourism, agriculture, and manufacturing. The organisation’s dedication to empowering small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) is particularly noteworthy, as it provides them with essential tools and resources to expand their reach while fostering inclusive economic progress. A cornerstone of ProDominicana’s approach is its emphasis on sustainability, evident in its advocacy for green investments and environmentally responsible practices. The organisation works tirelessly to create an enabling environment for business by championing regulatory reforms and significantly enhancing the nation’s investment appeal. ProDominicana has developed innovative programmes and services tailored to the specific needs of investors and exporters, ensuring the Dominican Republic remains competitive in the global economy. Its initiatives not only boost growth but also align with principles of social equity and environmental stewardship, further cementing its role as a leader in sustainable regional development. The CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates ProDominicana - Centro de Exportación e Inversión de la República Dominicana on winning the 2024 award for Outstanding Contribution to Sustainable Economic Growth (Caribbean).</p>
