# Schafer Cullen Capital Management: Innovators in Long-Term Value Equity Strategies USA 2025

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12977-schafer-cullen-capital-management-innovators-in-long-term-value-equity-strategie.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12984" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/SC.jpg" alt="SC" width="800" height="623" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Schafer Cullen Capital Management has built a reputation for disciplined, long-term value investing, backed by over five decades of expertise. The firm applies a rigorous valuation-based approach, emphasising price-to-earnings, price-to-book, and dividend yield as core selection criteria. Its strategy is designed to withstand market volatility, smoothing returns over rolling five-year periods while avoiding short-term speculation. Historical analysis of market cycles informs its investment philosophy, demonstrating that disciplined value investing outperforms over time, particularly in periods following speculative bubbles. Schafer Cullen’s research underscores the importance of maintaining a contrarian perspective, particularly when markets become overheated, as seen in previous eras such as the Nifty Fifty and the Tech Bubble. The firm’s commitment to fundamental analysis and investor discipline has resulted in a strategy that adapts across economic environments, including downturns. With a global outlook, it integrates international and emerging market opportunities where valuations offer compelling long-term prospects. Schafer Cullen also acknowledges the role of dividend growth, demonstrating how consistent dividend increases provide resilience in challenging markets. The firm’s leadership emphasises that successful investing is rooted in patience, valuation awareness, and a willingness to hold positions through economic cycles rather than reacting to short-term trends. As a result, Schafer Cullen has cultivated a loyal investor base that values stability, disciplined decision-making, and a clear focus on long-term wealth accumulation. The CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates Schafer Cullen Capital Management on winning the 2025 award for Innovators in Long-Term Value Equity Strategies (USA).</p>
