# BJC: Outstanding Eco-Responsible Business Leader Southeast Asia 2024

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12928-bjc-outstanding-eco-responsible-business-leader-southeast-asia-2024.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12929" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/BJC.jpg" alt="BJC" width="400" height="168" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">With a 140 years history, Berli Jucker Public Company Limited (BJC) is a stalwart in Thailand's economic landscape, significantly contributing to its trading, manufacturing, and service sectors. A partnership between the Berli and Jucker families, BJC swiftly became one of the country’s foremost trading conglomerates. Its early engagements included rice milling, mining, timber, shipping and importing, laying a foundation for Thailand's industrialisation drive. BJC evolved into a pivotal player in the nation's import and export domain, propelling itself into many diversified sectors, particularly in the post-World War II era. At the heart of BJC's endeavours lies a visionary commitment to fostering sustainable development and better living standards. Guided by a culture of customer-centricity and data-driven decision-making, BJC positions itself as the premier partner for sustainable growth. Its mission is underpinned by a dedication to delivering superior goods and services through innovative solutions, while pursuing sustainable prosperity for its myriad stakeholders. Under the stewardship of seasoned professionals who champion the principles of free trade and exemplary corporate governance, BJC remains resolutely accountable to all its many stakeholders. Embracing a profound ethos of integrity, innovation, and sustainability, BJC stands firm as a beacon of progress, deeply rooted in Thai society. Its enduring commitment to excellence and environmental stewardship underscores its pivotal role in driving Thailand's economic advancement while safeguarding the well-being of its populace and the environment alike. The panel confirms BJC as winner of the 2024 award: Outstanding Eco-Responsible Business Leader (Southeast Asia).</p>
