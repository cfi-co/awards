# Krungthai Bank PCL: Best Social Impact Bank Thailand 2025

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12903-krungthai-bank-pcl-best-social-impact-bank-thailand-2025.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-8660" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Krungthai.jpg" alt="Krungthai" width="500" height="147" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Krungthai Bank PCL plays a pivotal role in driving sustainable economic and social development in Thailand through its inclusive financial solutions and commitment to digital innovation. As a leading financial institution, the bank prioritises accessibility and financial literacy, ensuring that underserved communities and small businesses can benefit from tailored banking services. Its extensive digital banking ecosystem enhances financial inclusion, while strategic partnerships with government agencies and private enterprises facilitate nationwide socio-economic growth. Krungthai Bank integrates environmental, social, and governance (ESG) principles into its operations, reinforcing its dedication to responsible banking. By leveraging advanced technology and data-driven insights, the bank continuously refines its product offerings to meet evolving societal needs. Recent innovations, such as its blockchain-based savings bonds platform and real-time payment solutions, further enhance financial security and transactional convenience for individuals and businesses. Its proactive approach to corporate social responsibility extends beyond financial services, encompassing community engagement and sustainability initiatives that align with national development goals. Through robust risk management and transparent governance, Krungthai Bank fosters trust among stakeholders while maintaining a resilient and highly adaptable business model. The institution’s unwavering focus on ethical banking and sustainable impact has solidified its reputation as a key driver of positive transformation in Thailand’s financial sector. CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates Krungthai Bank PCL on winning the 2025 award for Best Social Impact Bank (Thailand).</p>
