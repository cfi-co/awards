# CORDET: Best Alternative Credit Investor United Kingdom 2025

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `12989-cordet-best-alternative-credit-investor-united-kingdom-2025.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-12991" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Cordet.jpg" alt="Cordet" width="500" height="84" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">CORDET provides flexible, long-term financing solutions to mid-market businesses across Northern and Western Europe, with a strong emphasis on sustainable and responsible investment practices. The firm has demonstrated resilience and adaptability in a competitive alternative lending landscape, navigating market fluctuations without compromising its investment strategy. In 2024, CORDET maintained a highly active portfolio, culminating in an exceptionally strong fourth-quarter performance. The firm has expanded its presence in the Nordic region, executing a notable transaction in Norway despite the challenges posed by the dominance of local banks and regional mini-bonds. CORDET’s approach prioritises deep sector expertise, strong borrower relationships, and a risk-conscious investment framework, ensuring capital deployment aligns with long-term value creation. The firm has also made strides in institutionalising its investment process, reducing reliance on individuals and implementing structured sourcing responsibilities. Furthermore, CORDET continues to develop its ESG commitments, with its forthcoming fund designated under Article 8, reinforcing its dedication to sustainable investment. Internally, the firm fosters a high-calibre talent pool, with key team members receiving industry recognition. Efforts to enhance investor communication and engagement have been a priority, ensuring transparency and consistency in reporting. Looking ahead, fundraising for its third fund remains a core focus, with plans to expand its investor base and achieve a more geographically balanced portfolio. CORDET’s disciplined approach and strategic positioning cement its reputation as a trusted investment partner. The CFI.co Judging Panel congratulates CORDET on winning the 2025 award for Best Alternative Credit Investor (United Kingdom).</p>
