# Wealth Club: Best HNW Investment Platform UK 2021

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `9227-wealth-club-best-hnw-investment-platform-uk-2021.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-9228" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/WealthClub.jpg" alt="WealthClub" width="500" height="92" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Wealth Club is a leading alternative investment platform exclusively for high net worth and sophisticated investors. Wealth Club specialises in government-endorsed, tax-incentivised solutions, including venture capital trusts (it is the largest non-advisory broker with a 19% market share), inheritance tax portfolios, enterprise and seed enterprise investment schemes (EIS and SEIS) and alternative investment market individual savings accounts (AIM ISAs). The company has some 7,000 clients and 35,000 members active on the platform. Wealth Club’s founding team brings leadership experience from the financial services industry, notably Hargreaves Lansdown, where Wealth Club CEO Alex Davies served as a board director. Since launch in 2016 Wealth Club clients have invested £560m through its platform. The Wealth Club platform gives clients control over investing, with insights from in-house teams of analysts and researchers as well as CEO interviews. Technology and medtech are areas of high interest, and investment minimums start at as low as £10,000 for single company opportunities. The company has laid a solid foundation towards the goal of building a £1bn business — from the ground up. The CFI.co judging panel announces Wealth Club as winner of the 2021 award for Best HNW Investment Platform (UK).</p>
