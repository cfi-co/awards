# Carne Group Financial Services: Best Fund Services Team Ireland 2020

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `8939-carne-group-financial-services-best-fund-services-team-ireland-2020.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-8940" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Carne.jpg" alt="Carne" width="200" height="319" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Carne Group is trusted by the world’s leading asset managers, insurance companies, pension funds and wealth managers to manage their governance, compliance, regulatory and substance requirements. Carne, founded in 2004, is a global leader of outsourced fund management solutions. It is headquartered in Ireland with offices in the financial districts of Luxembourg, Switzerland, the Cayman Islands, Portugal, the UK, the US, and the Channel Islands. Carne deploys a team of 270 professionals – comprised of 25 nationalities, and including 30 accountants and 10 lawyers – to support a roster of some 500 clients. Carne has achieved gender parity in its diverse team, which fosters long-term relationships with clients. It delivers innovative management solutions for everything from data and due diligence to compliance, risks and operations. Carne combines over 16 years of industry and local expertise with institutional best-practice and is a governance and substance provider for Asset Managers with more than $1tn in AUM. Its management, governance and fund services are supported by Carne’s industry leading technology and risk driven platform, CORR (Compliance, Oversight, Risk &amp; Reporting). The CFI.co judging panel is pleased to present Carne Group Financial Services with the 2020 award for the Best Fund Services Team (Ireland).</p>
