# Adepa: Best Global Fund Administration Team Europe 2021

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `9722-adepa-best-global-fund-administration-team-europe-2021-2.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-9723" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Adepa.jpg" alt="Adepa" width="400" height="92" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Over the past 40 years, Adepa has assembled a multicultural team with deep experience in investing, banking and financial services. The group is headquartered in Luxembourg with offices in Italy, Spain and Chile. The team is multi-lingual and works with clients across the globe, putting no geographic limits on the development of international products and services. It’s equally adept with liquid and illiquid strategies. The group offers a comprehensive range of structured solutions for independent financial advisors, asset managers, private equity real estate, venture capitalists, family offices and banking organisations. The group has evolved from its origins as an asset manager to include business process outsourcing (BPO) services that ensure cost efficiency. BPO services can be contracted for selected processes or to cover all administrative areas. The group has developed a client leadership approach to ensure the efficient management of business relationships. Dedicated team members serve as client leaders, acting as the intermediary between client and company. Client leaders work with a limited number of accounts and funds to allow personalised attention without time constraints. Adepa gives employees the autonomy and independence to act in clients’ best interests, while providing the organisational and tech support to deliver consistent results. The group constantly encourages employees’ growth and entrepreneurial spirit and inspires client loyalty by placing their needs at the centre of operations. The CFI.co judging panel announces Adepa as 2021 award winner for Best Global Fund Administration Team (Europe).</p>
