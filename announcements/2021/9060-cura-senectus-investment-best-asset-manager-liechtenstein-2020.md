# Cura & Senectus Investment: Best Asset Manager Liechtenstein 2020

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `9060-cura-senectus-investment-best-asset-manager-liechtenstein-2020.json`.

[caption id="attachment_9115" align="aligncenter" width="768"]<img class="size-large wp-image-9115" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/MVZ_CFI-768x1024.jpg" alt="Michel van Zanten" width="768" height="1024" /> Michel van Zanten[/caption]
<p style="text-align: justify;">The people make the difference at Cura &amp; Senectus Investment, an asset and wealth manager based in the financial centre of Liechtenstein. The go-ahead principality nestled between Germany, Austria and Switzerland gives the firm an advantage in the recruitment of top talent. It has assembled a team with deep investment expertise and complementary background in many asset classes including private equity, digital assets, real estate and alternative investments. Cura &amp; Senectus Investment works with asset allocators, family offices and wealthy individuals and has build a track record of delivering strong returns. It focuses on the implementation of high performance investments and stayed ahead of the competition with, amongst others, the early adoption of digital investments and implementation of best practice principles. Liechtenstein was one of the first countries in the European Economic Area to implement the AIFMD – the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Directive. This regulatory framework for alternative funds (hedge funds, investment trusts and private equity) was adopted shortly before Cura &amp; Senectus Investment launched. The firm follows a strict code of conduct to safeguard clients assets and investments. Cura &amp; Senectus Investment prides itself on strong risk management skills and focus on due diligence, oversight and regulatory reporting. It offers services in Asset Management, fund set-up and structuring, establishing investor profiles and family office services. This is a firm that doesn’t rest on its laurels, and 2021 will see a roll out of new products. The CFI.co judging panel announces Cura &amp; Senectus as the winner of the 2020 award for Best Asset Manager (Liechtenstein).</p>
