# March Asset Management: Best Thematic Funds Management Team Spain 2021

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `9218-march-asset-management-best-thematic-funds-management-team-spain-2021.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-9219" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/BancaMarch.jpg" alt="BancaMarch" width="500" height="152" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">As part of an unlisted, family-owned group, March Asset Management (March AM) is not beholden to shareholder or market pressures, leaving it free to focus on long-term value creation for clients. The firm was founded in 2000 to serve as the investment arm of the March Group, one of the great European banking dynasties. March AM was the first boutique to introduce thematic investment funds in Spain over a decade ago — and it’s now a specialist. Funds centre around concerns for the planet and family businesses. The firm maintains low debt ratios by focusing on value creation. It has some €6bn in AUM and is developing value-added thematic funds. March AM’s senior leaders are recognised players with over 35 years of combined experience and deep industry expertise. The team works in cohesive harmony and takes pride in deploying proven risk-management skills. March AM’s investment philosophy centres around value creation and asset protection over the long term. It seeks out pricing inefficiencies that present opportunities for engaged, direct and active investors — then allocates portfolio assets and selects companies with tactical precision. March AM has developed a sustainable and responsible investment policy that ensures the decision-making process includes ESG criteria as well as quantitative and qualitative financial considerations. It influences corporate governance decisions with proxy votes and engages with investee companies to drive ESG performance. The CFI.co judging panel presents March Asset Management with the 2021 award for Best Thematic Funds Management Team (Spain).</p>
