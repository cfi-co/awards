# Taurus Group: Best Digital Asset Infrastructure Provider Switzerland 2021

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `8890-taurus-group-best-digital-asset-infrastructure-provider-switzerland-2021.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-8891" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Taurus.jpg" alt="Taurus" width="400" height="133" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Taurus Group has earned the trust of global financial institutions — in record time. It was established in 2018 in Geneva to launch one of the world’s first regulated digital asset exchanges. It built the most complete digital asset platform ever seen — from the ground up. Taurus has a full-service solution for banks, exchanges, technology providers and asset owners, as well as managers with a single platform for the issuing, onboarding, management and booking of tokenised securities and digital currencies, covering any type of asset on any standard. Clients can digitalise and tokenise private assets and manage cryptocurrency assets within a simple, secure, next-generation infrastructure. Taurus offers four battle-tested and future-proof products: PROTECT, CAPITAL, EXPLORER and TRADE. PROTECT is a custody platform supporting crypto currencies, tokenised assets and digital currencies. CAPITAL enables all-in-one asset servicing operations. EXPLORER provides SaaS blockchain nodes and indexing to help clients reduce costs and scale faster, while TRADE offers competitive and transparent pricing across a wide asset coverage. Taurus engineers make up 70 percent of the Swiss company’s staff. It has distinguished itself with expertise that crosses into financial services and regulations. Taurus has claimed more than half of its home market share and will use future funding rounds to fuel its expansion plans. The CFI.co judging panel presents Taurus Group with the 2021 award for Best Digital Asset Infrastructure Provider (Switzerland).</p>
