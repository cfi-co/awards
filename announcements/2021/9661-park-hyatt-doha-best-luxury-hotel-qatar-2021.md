# Park Hyatt Doha: Best Luxury Hotel Qatar 2021

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `9661-park-hyatt-doha-best-luxury-hotel-qatar-2021.json`.

[caption id="attachment_9662" align="aligncenter" width="900"]<img class="wp-image-9662 size-large" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/CFI.co-Award-Park-Hyatt-Doha-1024x651.jpg" alt="CFI.co-Award-Park-Hyatt-Doha" width="900" height="572" /> Deep Kumar Sindhi (Director of Sales and Marketing), Stefan Gaessler (General Manager), and CFI.co's Chairman Tor Svensson[/caption]
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Park Hyatt Doha is the best luxury hotel in Qatar based on the sum and combination of the following six criteria according to CFI.co’s judging panel:</strong></p>

<ul style="text-align: justify;">
 	<li><strong>Leadership and Management Team:</strong> The Hyatt veteran hotelier, Stefan Gaessler, has successfully spearheaded the hotel’s preopening and opening, establishing Park Hyatt as a synonym of luxury in Qatar and Doha’s fashionable district, Msheireb. Working with the team of experienced hospitality professionals, Stefan ensures smooth and efficient operations, achievement of the hotel’s financial goals, and Park Hyatt leading positioning on local and international markets.</li>
 	<li><strong>Guest Services:</strong> A reference in worldwide hospitality, Park Hyatt Doha follows the highest standards and offers exclusive experiences to its guests across rooms, F&amp;B, spa and events. The team of passionate and highly trained hospitality experts works tirelessly to ensure excellence at all levels.</li>
 	<li><strong>Prime Location:</strong> Mixing tradition with world class modern culture, Msheireb downtown is Doha’s most sought-after destination, where culture, fashion and luxury meet. Jewel of the district, Barahat Msheireb town square is just a step from the hotel, and features beautiful facades and breath-taking evening lightings.</li>
 	<li><strong>The Building and the Rooms:</strong> Designed by the hotel expert award-winning architect Jun Aizaki, Park Hyatt Doha is a work of art, created with the most noble materials and embodying the spirit of finesse in the architecture, blending modernity with local traditions. Hotel’s rooms feature an immaculate décor, while offering the utmost comfort, space, and light.</li>
 	<li><strong>Fine Dining:</strong> High gastronomy is at the heart of Park Hyatt experience. Opus, led by the French Michelin-starred chef Jean-<em>François</em> Roquette, brilliantly marries Qatari and French cuisine in a unique and elaborate menu. Sora is Park Hyatt gourmet Japanese cuisine destination, offering the finest seafood and meet gastronomic creations, a selection of original cocktails, and spectacular view of the West Bay skyline. Anis, Park Hyatt’s all-day-dining café, invites guests to enjoy healthy snacks and drinks in cozy and warm interiors, or at a terrace overlooking a lovely pedestrian street of Msheireb district.</li>
 	<li><strong>Sustainability and alignment with SDGs:</strong> Committed to support Sustainable Development Goals and actively taking part in global environmental transition, Hyatt launched a dedicated Environmental, Social and Governance platform ‘World of Care’. Part of the programme an designed in line with the highest sustainable design standards, Park Hyatt Doha continuously implements environmentally friendly strategies in its operations, aligned with Msheireb sustainable district requirements.</li>
</ul>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Since 1957, Park Hyatt Doha follows a proud tradition of creating an intimate luxury hotel experience, and offers the most unique and sumptuous hospitality experience in Qatar.</p>
