# Eden McCallum: Best Management Consultancy Team Europe 2022

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `10579-eden-mccallum-best-management-consultancy-team-europe-2022.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-10578" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/EdenMcCallum.jpg" alt="Eden McCallum" width="400" height="140" />

&nbsp;
<p style="text-align: justify;">Eden McCallum is a European management consultancy launched by Liann Eden and Dena McCallum over 20 years ago. The cofounders, who shared North American upbringings and European careers at McKinsey, wanted to introduce more flexibility into the consultancy process without compromising on calibre. The firm works with more than 2,300 independent consultants and 500 clients worldwide, including a third of the FTSE 100 and 60 of the global <em>Fortune</em> 500. An experienced in-house team of partners, managers, analysts and client directors — around 70 in total — collaborate across Eden McCallum’s offices in London and Amsterdam. Eden McCallum has been praised by clients and partners alike, and repeat business accounts for 85 percent of its work. Positive word-of-mouth and Statistica surveys helped the firm to claim top bragging rights in the <em>Financial Times</em> 2022 ranking of leading management consultants in the UK. Eden McCallum has also been featured in <em>The Economist</em> and cited by academics at Harvard Business School and London Business School for pioneering a consultancy approach that prioritises results, value and client ownership. Eden McCallum assembles teams of in-house staff and independent consultants according to each project’s needs, from strategy to transformation. These teams work closely alongside clients to set out and achieve their goals, with the highest strategic impact. The CFI.co judging panel announces Eden McCallum as the 2022 award winner for Best Management Consultancy Team (Europe).</p>
