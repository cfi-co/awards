# BNP Paribas REIM : Best Real Estate lnvestment Manager Europe 2022

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-10318" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/BNP-Baribas-REIM.jpg" alt="BNP-Baribas-REIM" width="495" height="102" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">BNP Paribas Real Estate Investment Management (REIM) is part of BNP Paribas Real Estate, a leading European property and investment manager with a well-established portfolio and strategic focus on sustainable growth. The company ensures client proximity and local expertise with a workforce of 5,000 employees and a presence in 30 countries across Europe, the Middle East and Asia. It’s a people-centered business, with responsible practices that prioritise employee empowerment, community benefits and investor returns. lt assists clients with every step of the property lifecycle, from development and transaction to consulting, valuation and management of properties and investments. BNP Paribas REIM has identified eight key market transitions that will allow it to create opportunities white addressing future urban challenges. lt pushes for flexible community design of residential and commercial spaces and tech-enhanced buildings that generate and incorporate data to improve users' well-being and quality of life. Amenities-rich properties with human-centric spaces and services promote community cohesion - while also boosting property demand and value. BNP Paribas Real Estate lnvestment Management is the business line dedicated to Investment Management activities. It offers a range of real estate funds and investment solutions that meet the needs of international investors across Europe on all asset typologies. With a local presence in seven countries, BNP Paribas REIM employs nearly 360 employees offering solutions to more than 150,000 clients, institutional investors and individuals. At the end of 2021, BNP Paribas REIM managed €29.7bn assets in Europe. In 2020, it launched the first European institutional real estate fund aligned with the COP 21 Paris Agreement. The European Impact Property Fund (EIPF), which raised €300 million in equity commitments within a year of its launch, allows investors to reconcile portfolio performance and diversification with ecological priorities. BNP Paribas REIM invests in ail property sectors, from corporate offices to residential, hotels, healthcare facilities and logistics. The CFl.co judging panel presents BNP Paribas REIM with the 2022 award for BNP Paribas REIM (Europe).</p>
