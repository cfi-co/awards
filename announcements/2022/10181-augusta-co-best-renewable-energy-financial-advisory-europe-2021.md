# Augusta & Co: Best Renewable Energy Financial Advisory Europe 2021

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `10181-augusta-co-best-renewable-energy-financial-advisory-europe-2021.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-10182" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Augusta-and-Co.jpg" alt="Augusta &amp; Co" width="500" height="124" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Augusta &amp; Co is fuelling the renewable energy transition. The London-based company was founded by a team of senior investment bankers 20 years ago, and the leadership — and ownership — of the company is still shared among senior staff. Augusta &amp; Co has gained in-depth technical expertise throughout a history of financial advisory representing some 22 gigawatts of renewable energy assets and €12bn in equity capital raised across 100 transactions. It promises independent, unbiased financial advice that’s free from conflicts of interest and that supports a decarbonised future. It has helped clients to chart optimal funding routes and investment strategies across a range of renewable energy technologies: onshore and offshore wind, solar, hydro, biomass, landfill gas, biofuels, marine generation and energy storage systems. Augusta &amp; Co has recruited some of the world’s smartest minds to its 35+-person workforce, which ranks as one of the largest specialised teams in the European renewable energy sector. The company is the top financial advisor for utility-scale wind and hydro energy assets in Europe, and it harnesses deep sector experience to unlock decarbonisation benefits for all businesses. It works with a global network of thousands of industrial leaders and renewable energy investors to provide clients with execution-focused advice and the most trustworthy and nuanced market insights. For a company assisting businesses to find a cleaner, greener path, the CFI.co judging panel declares Augusta &amp; Co as the 2021 Best Renewable Energy Financial Advisory (Europe) award winner.</p>
