# Lifecare International: Best Corporate Health Insurance Brokerage UAE 2021

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `10243-lifecare-international-best-corporate-health-insurance-brokerage-uae-2021.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-10244" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Lifecare.jpg" alt="Lifecare" width="400" height="265" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Lifecare International has grown to be one of the leading insurance brokerages in the Middle East &amp; Africa, specializing in Health Insurance. With 6 offices and employing over 125 people across the UAE, Kenya, and Qatar, Lifecare is a family-run business serving its core purpose: <em>to help everyone protect and care for those that matter most. </em></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Lifecare offers a range of turnkey insurance solutions covering health, life, critical illness, and general insurance lines, addressing the needs of individuals, SMEs, and large corporate clients. As an insurance partner relied on by over 1400 businesses, Lifecare takes the time to identify the risks facing each client and tailors a unique insurance scheme to suit their organizational and financial needs.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">An independent, ISO-9001 certified company and one of the first GDPR compliant insurance brokers, Lifecare has established a 24/7 Health Assistance and Concierge Desk. Working with best in breed providers such as international SOS, Bupa Cromwell, and King’s College Hospital, it provides a level of service, typically reserved only for Fortune-500 companies.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Lifecare calls this program of care, Cura, an award-winning health and wellness concierge that custom-crafts a wellness journey for their clients from a range of over 20 allied partners. With Cura, clients get access to priority hospital bookings, telehealth consultations, medical and security travel assistance, wellness coaching, emotional support, talks, webinars, and more. Through claims analytics and risk assessments, it aims to understand where the major health risks lie and then implements impactful cost containment measures and controls to ensure sustainable premiums in the future. During the pandemic, where health was at the forefront, the company partnered with HealthX and TruDoc, to provide complimentary tele-health services to clients.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Lifecare continuously re-invests back into the company to fuel innovation and constant improvement. As an example, LifeNet, the company’s proprietary digital policy management platform, streamlines the administration of medical policies, reducing time and creating efficiencies by 60 percent.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co judging panel announces Lifecare International Insurance as the 2021 award winner for Best Corporate Health Insurance Brokerage (UAE).</p>
