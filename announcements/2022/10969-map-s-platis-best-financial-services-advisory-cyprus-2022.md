# MAP S.Platis: Best Financial Services Advisory Cyprus 2022

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `10969-map-s-platis-best-financial-services-advisory-cyprus-2022.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-10970" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/mapslatis-jpg.webp" alt="mapslatis" width="183" height="156" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Over the past two decades, innovation has been key to the success of <a href="https://www.mapsplatis.com/">MAP S.Platis</a>. It’s an integral part of the Group’s DNA, permeating all its products and services.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">MAP S.Platis is a leading financial services consulting group with clients including regulators, banks, funds and fund managers, investment firms, brokers, fintech firms, insurance firms and payment and electronic money institutions. Its expert team, which comprises over 400 high-calibre professionals, provides tailored solutions in licensing, regulatory compliance, risk management, internal audit, human resources, regulatory technology, information technology, business resilience, information security (including cybersecurity), governance, executive training, and innovation consulting to financial and other firms in the UK, Cyprus, and the EU.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">MAP S.Platis works closely with regulators and the financial services industry to bridge the gap and identify trends, and also challenges the misconception of compliance as a business obstacle by identifying issues and solutions with passion, creativity, and professionalism.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This pioneering Group has also developed comprehensive and cutting-edge regulatory reporting solutions, provided through its regulatory technology firm, MAP FinTech, and has also established the European Institute of Management and Finance (EIMF), a leading provider of premium courses, professional and executive education, eLearning, and tailored learning solutions with an emphasis on the financial and professional services sector.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co judging panel welcomes MAP S.Platis to the awards programme circle of winners, as <strong>Best Financial Services Advisory (Cyprus, 2022).</strong></p>
