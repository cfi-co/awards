# Crown Jewels Consultants: Best Financial Market Data Services UK 2022

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `10722-crown-jewels-consultants-best-financial-market-data-services-uk-2022.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-10723" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/CJC.jpg" alt="CJC" width="400" height="160" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Crown Jewels Consultants (CJC) understands data and is dedicated to helping the global financial services sector harness its potential. The company has established a presence in four key financial hubs — including London headquarters and offices in New York, Hong Kong and Singapore — to manage and support the complex, real-time, market data distribution technologies used in the global trade cycle. CJC credits its top-of-class status to a stellar team driven by professionals with deep industry experience and talented young recruits. The company has created an academy to train the next generation in the holistic skills needed to maximise clients’ operational efficiency. It continues to develop marketing data services for the cloud and has brought on people with strong capabilities in IT and engineering. CJC has focused on the financial services sector for over 20 years and has earned the trust — and repeat business — of many financial institutions worldwide. It offers consultancy, professional services and cloud-based tools to optimise and support the efficient distribution and commercial management of market data, helping clients reduce enterprise cost and mitigate risk. No two projects or clients are the same, so CJC tailors services according to need, whether that requires a one-off review or a fully managed service plan. CJC pioneered market data platform hosting and is now championing the technologies of future data ecosystems. The CFI.co judging panel announces Crown Jewels Consultants as the winner of the 2022 award for Best Financial Market Data Services (UK).</p>
