# Raiffeisen Centrobank AG: Best Structured Products Bank CEE 2022

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `10807-raiffeisen-centrobank-ag-best-structured-products-bank-cee-2022.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-4975" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Raifeisen.jpg" alt="Raiffeisen" width="324" height="91" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">Over the past 25 years, Raiffeisen Centrobank (RCB) has distinguished itself among the top certificates houses in Austria and Central and Eastern Europe (CEE). The Vienna-based investment bank has solidified its position as the regional competence centre for structured products and equity trading. RCB takes care in explaining the opportunities and risks involved in securities investments. It highlights structured products as an ideal complement to a traditional investment portfolio, providing customised access to hard-to-reach asset classes. RCB presents potential investors with thousands of products organised by category and currency, including 357 new issues and nine currently in subscription. Conscientious investors appreciate RCB’s range of sustainable investment products, which provide customers with the means to shore up their own prosperity, future-proof their portfolio and play a hand in the design of a better future. Clients can invest in certificates covering specific topics, technologies, industries and trends. Some products offer potential returns up to eight percent. Others feature safety mechanisms that protect 100 percent of the investment. The bank, a subsidiary or Raiffeisen Bank International, is constantly strengthening its position in core markets such as the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary as well as in the Balkan region. The certificates business of RCB will be integrated into RBI in December 2022 to further develop and grow the business in the CEE region as well as in Austria. The CFI.co judging panel has followed the bank’s progress over the past five years — and is pleased to reaffirm RCB’s champion status in the award category for Best Structured Products Bank (CEE 2022).</p>
