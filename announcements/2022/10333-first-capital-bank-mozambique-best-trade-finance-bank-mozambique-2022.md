# First Capital Bank Mozambique: Best Trade Finance Bank Mozambique 2022

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `10333-first-capital-bank-mozambique-best-trade-finance-bank-mozambique-2022.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-10334" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/First-Capital-Bank.jpg" alt="First Capital Bank" width="400" height="75" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">First Capital Bank Mozambique provides exceptional care for commercial and corporate clients, regardless of their size. The bank offers a range of off-the-shelf products and customised solutions to catalyse the growth and development of local businesses. First Capital Bank Mozambique has proven to be a reliable long-term partner for Corporates and SMEs, supporting them throughout times of success and struggle. The bank makes itself available to business clients, all of whom have the CEO’s number and are encouraged to reach out if necessary. First Capital Bank Mozambique promises rapid response times, approving substantial loans for select applicants within fast turnaround times. It’s upfront with financing denials, too, allowing unsuccessful applicants to explore other options without unnecessary delays. First Capital Bank Mozambique ensures proximity through a growing branch network, dedicated relationship managers and internet banking services. The bank engages with clients to understand their cross-border ambitions and help resolve any problems. It attempts to accommodate clients if things go sideways. First Capital Bank Mozambique provided support to pandemic-impacted clients and assisted with restructuring and middle-ground solutions when regional conflict caused disruption in gas projects. First Capital Bank has established operations in five Southern African Development Community countries — Mozambique, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Botswana and Malawi, where the group is headquartered. Each division pulls from group resources and connections to provide clients with solutions that are relevant throughout the regions. The CFI.co judging panel announces First Capital Bank Mozambique as the 2022 award winner for Best Trade Finance Bank (Mozambique).</p>
