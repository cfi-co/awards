# Best Investment Bank, Saudi Arabia - NCB Capital

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `235-best-investment-bank-saudi-arabia-ncb-capital.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="  aligncenter wp-image-236" title="ncbc-logo" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2012/10/ncbc-logo.jpg" alt="" width="150" height="150" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The judges applauded NCB’s 2011 M&amp;A deal ($500 million) consolidating six construction companies. </strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">This was the largest M&amp;A deal in the region from Saudi Arabia’s biggest asset manager ($11.75 billion). This was the first subsidiary of a Saudi Bank to be licenced by the Capital Market Authority for Investment Management and Asset Management in Saudi Arabia.</p>
