# Hana Bank wins Best Private Bank Korea 2012

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `111-hana-bank-wins-best-private-bank-korea-2012.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-1883" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/hb.jpg" alt="hb" width="357" height="141" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Hana, one of the biggest and best run banks in Korea is a subsidiary of Hana Financial Group. </strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">In 2011, HFG entered into what the judging panel termed as a ‘very promising alliance’ with Indonesia’s largest private bank. The judging panel also commented on the Bank’s rather humble presentation of its strong credentials as well as ‘an obvious and continuous concern to enhance the client banking experience’.</p>
