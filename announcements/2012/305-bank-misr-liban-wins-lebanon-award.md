# Bank Misr Liban Wins Lebanon Award

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `305-bank-misr-liban-wins-lebanon-award.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong><img class="  wp-image-306 aligncenter" title="bmlLogo" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/bmlLogo.jpg" alt="" width="201" height="74" /></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>We are pleased to announce Bank Misr Liban (BML) as Best Commercial Bank Lebanon, 2012.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The Bank and its stakeholders are now seeing the benefits of the restructuring that has taken place over the past few years. This restructuring has left BML not only in a better financial position but also with improved levels of corporate governance and transparency. In the view of CFI.co’s judging panel, these achievements - combined with the very strong local knowledge built up since 1929 - means that the Bank is perfectly positioned to provide safe and very high levels of service to its customers and deliver just rewards to all its stakeholders.</p>
