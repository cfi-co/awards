# Three Principles and Strong Corporate Leadership win Award for Mitsubishi

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `210-three-principles-and-strong-corporate-leadership-win-award-for-mitsubishi.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><a href="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/mitsubishi.jpg"><img class="  wp-image-211 aligncenter" title="mitsubishi" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/mitsubishi-279x300.jpg" alt="" width="195" height="210" /></a></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Mitsubishi Corporation - the multinational conglomerate - refers to itself more humbly as a ‘community of independent companies’. </strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The independents are tied together by their wholehearted acceptance of the ‘Three Principles’, namely corporate social responsibility; integrity and fairness; and global understanding through business. The Mitsubishi Foundation was established in 1969 to mark the centenary of the organisation and all those independents are successors to a  shipping company founded in Japan in 1870. The judging panel recognised the significant efforts of Mitsubishi in helping Japan recover from the 2011 Earthquake after a 10 billion yen donation to the cause. The judges describe Mitsubishi  as an outstanding example of corporate leadership in Asia.</p>
