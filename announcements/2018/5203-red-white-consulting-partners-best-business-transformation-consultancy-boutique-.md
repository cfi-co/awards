# Red & White Consulting Partners: Best Business Transformation Consultancy Boutique Singapore 2017

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `5203-red-white-consulting-partners-best-business-transformation-consultancy-boutique-.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-5204" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/RedWhite.jpg" alt="" width="500" height="181" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The business engineers of Red &amp; White Consulting Partners leverage both their knowledge and experience to deliver superior and transformative results for the firm’s clients. The Singapore company has established a solid track record for unlocking the potential of businesses: breaking through ceilings, broaching new markets, and upping the ante on corporate efficiency, adaptability, and branding.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Red &amp; White Consulting Partners adheres to a holistic approach to overcoming challenges, including the financial, business, and human aspects that together form the triad on which sustainable growth is based. The firm’s professionals encourage critical thinking, poking holes in established wisdom in order to source innovative ways of removing the barriers that hamper business development.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The firm organises regular workshop programmes that allow clients to analyse their attitudes to business and detect synergies that improve overall efficiency. Red &amp; White Consulting Partners was set up in 2013 by two veteran bankers who believe in the power of data-driven solutions to common issues facing businesses as they expand and adapt to changing market conditions. The firm has worked with clients across the world and in all sectors of the economy from manufacturing and financials to airlines, NGOs and retailers.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co judging panel commends Red &amp; White Consulting Partners on its hands-on no-nonsense approach to business engineering. The firm consistently offers practical solutions that closely fit the client’s profile and deliver tangible results. For the second consecutive year, the judges agree to recognise the firm’s achievements. Red &amp; White Consulting Partners is named winner of the 2017 Best Business Transformation Consultancy Boutique Award.</p>
