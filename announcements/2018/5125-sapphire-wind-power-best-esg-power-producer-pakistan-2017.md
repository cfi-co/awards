# Sapphire Wind Power: Best ESG Power Producer Pakistan 2017

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `5125-sapphire-wind-power-best-esg-power-producer-pakistan-2017.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-5126" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Sapphire.jpg" alt="" width="500" height="80" /></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>Completing all its projects on-time and on-budget, Sapphire Wind Power has taken the lead in the currently unfolding transformation of the energy market. The Pakistan company adheres to a business model that allows for the creation of tangible value. Its projects benefit from on-site oversight by expert professionals and are unfailingly considered bankable. In fact, thanks to its solid reputation Sapphire Wind Power is able to maintain a number of projects running simultaneously whilst meeting – and exceeding – expectations.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Committed to deliver clean, green, and affordable energy solutions anywhere in the country, Sapphire Wind Power follows well-established international best practices that support, amongst others, capacity building and skill development. Additionally, the company implemented a robust corporate social responsibility (CSR) policy framework to reach out to communities and lend its knowhow to help lift thousands out of poverty.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Sapphire Wind Power works in close harmony with a number of international development financiers and agencies. The company is known for its corporate transparency and adherence to the latest ESG (environmental, social, and governance) standards. It is currently working on a number of projects such as a triple 50MW facility in the Gharo-Jhimpir wind corridor in the southeast of the country. Sapphire Wind Power is also involved in the design of a 40MW sized hybrid wind/solar power generator.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CFI.co judging panel is pleased to note and recognise the company’s dedication to furthering the renewable energy revolution and declares Sapphire Wind Power winner of the 2017 Best ESG Power Producer Pakistan Award.</p>
