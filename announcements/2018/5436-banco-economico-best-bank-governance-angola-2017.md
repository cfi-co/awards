# Banco Económico: Best Bank Governance Angola 2017

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `5436-banco-economico-best-bank-governance-angola-2017.json`.

<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong><img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-5438" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/BancoEconomico.jpg" alt="" width="400" height="107" /></strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The future is made today. To Banco Económico that means laying a solid foundation, grounded in common sense and shaped by ethics, that can support and sustain long-term growth. Founded in 2014 but built on a corporate legacy stretching back to the 1800s, Angola’s Banco Económico possesses an institutional skillset matched by few.</strong></p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The bank’s operational wherewithal is backed up by an exceptionally strong corporate governance framework that includes carefully matched checks and balances – put in place to ensure both optimum performance and full compliance with domestic and international regulations. By weaving a network of interlocking committees, Banco Económico manages to maintain streamlined internal and external procedures that, in turn, enable the bank to respond swiftly and decisively to changing circumstance – an absolute must in Angola’s transitioning economy.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">By adhering to international best practices and maintaining a world-class corporate governance structure, Banco Económico has become a valued partner to global businesses operating in the country. The bank enjoys a nationwide reputation for excellence in customer service and operational efficiency, not least because of its sustained investment in technology.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Internally, Banco Económico keeps an open culture that prioritises collaboration and encourages innovation. The CFI.co judging panel agrees that the most resilient and profitable banks, paradoxically, often resemble glass houses: structural strength is derived from transparency. The judges congratulate Banco Económico with its achievements and declare the bank winner of the 2017 Best Bank Governance Angola Award.</p>
