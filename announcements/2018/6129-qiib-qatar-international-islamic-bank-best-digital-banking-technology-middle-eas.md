# QIIB (Qatar International Islamic Bank): Best Digital Banking Technology Middle East 2018

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `6129-qiib-qatar-international-islamic-bank-best-digital-banking-technology-middle-eas.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-6131" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/QIIB.png" alt="" width="349" height="83" />
<p style="text-align: justify;">QIIB (Qatar International Islamic Bank) — a trusted financial institution with corporate, retail, treasury, and investment expertise — puts customer needs at the forefront of its business model, seamlessly whilst integrating industry standard technology across its platforms. QIIB remains true to its traditional values while providing customers with the necessary tools to thrive in a modern society. Promising to “elevate customer service beyond all expectations,” QIIB employs leading FinTech solutions to support consumer and corporate clients on an individual, integrated platform, thus increasing returns, reducing risk, and improving business efficiency. QIIB’s convenient mobile banking puts the power of the bank in the palm of your hand, while its hassle-free Internet banking features enable companies to facilitate financial transactions and support business needs. The CFI.co judging panel commends QIIB for its modern adaptation of traditional Shariah-compliant banking, earning them the 2018 award for Best Digital Banking Technology Middle East.</p>
