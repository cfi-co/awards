# ASIT Biotech: Best Biopharmaceutical Corporate Governance Belgium 2018

> Verbatim archived copy. Canonical machine record: `6076-asit-biotech-best-biopharmaceutical-corporate-governance-belgium-2018.json`.

<img class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-6077" src="https://cfi.co/awards/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/ASIT.jpg" alt="" width="399" height="103" />
<p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>In the fast-moving Biopharma world, corporate governance can sometimes be low on the priority list and is sometime viewed as little more than a tick box exercise.</strong> As a result, many companies have failed to raise the capital they need to succeed. The CFI.co judging panel were seeking an example of a Biopharma that had used the implementation of high governance standards as an enabler for success. ASIT Biotech have given their corporate governance the same level of attention they give to their science. ASIT have been constantly evolving and refining their governance and have ensured the correct business/academic and executive/non-executive balance that in combination with strong product potential has helped deliver several rounds of funding. This year saw the board call in independent evaluation that will help them further strengthen their governance as they look to bring their breakthrough immunotherapy products to market in the near future. The CFI.co judging panel reflected on ASIT’s constant efforts to ensure the correct level of governance to deliver their innovative technology to market and are pleased to announce  ASIT Biotech as winner of the award Best Biopharmaceutical Corporate Governance Belgium 2018.</p>
